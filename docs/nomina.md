# Nómina (payroll)

- Module under `app/dashboard/nomina/`: **Periodos** (`nomina/periodos/`), **Procesar nómina** (`nomina/procesar/`) with per-employee **Detalle** (`nomina/procesar/[id_empleado]/`), **Comisiones** (`nomina/comisiones/`), **Horas extra** (`nomina/horas-extra/`, detection+authorization then payment), **Faltas** (`nomina/faltas/`), **Retardos** (`nomina/retardos/`), **Bonos** (`nomina/bonos/`: puntualidad, asistencia, extensión de turno as three tabs), **Parámetros fiscales** (`nomina/parametros-fiscales/`: yearly tax data the ISR uses), **PTU** (`nomina/ptu/`: profit-sharing distribution per company/year, paid in an extraordinary period) and **Días pendientes de pago** (`nomina/dias-pendientes/`: hand-captured days worked but never paid, settled in an ordinary period). `nomina/page.tsx` redirects to Periodos.
- A period can be **ordinary** (`tipo_periodo = 'O'`) or **extraordinary** (`'E'`), and an extraordinary one has a **concept**: Christmas bonus (`'A'`, "Aguinaldo") or PTU (`'P'`).
- **Concepts calculated**: base salary; commission for attended consultas, onychomycosis treatments and sold products (operativa only); authorized overtime (both payrolls); punctuality bonus, attendance bonus and shift-extension bonus of podólogos (operativa only); vacation premium and paid vacation days (both payrolls, each at its own daily salary); pending day payments (both payrolls, each at its own hand-captured daily rate, frozen at calculation). Base salary is reduced by unjustified absences and unjustified lateness of podólogos (half a day per severe delay plus configurable accumulation tiers), in both payrolls.
- **Deductions, fiscal payroll only**: ISR (weekly periods only — article-96 tariff on taxable base minus employment subsidy) and the worker's IMSS contribution (ordinary periods, any frequency, on the SBC typed in the employee record). Every fiscal row shows ISR, IMSS and net pay.
- **Each fiscal perception is its own row** with taxed/exempt amounts, via a generic engine reading the `payroll.perceptions` catalog (today: salary, fully taxed; double and triple overtime). ISR base = sum of taxed amounts. Not built: other bonuses, employer's IMSS, ISR of other frequencies, dispersion.
- Approval and payment (statuses 3/4), reopening a paid period (4→2) and hand-captured pending day payments are all built — see their own sections below.
- Lives in the `payroll` schema (separate from `RH`, like `BILLING`). Key tables besides `periods`/`period_employees`: `commission_tiers`; `treatment_commission_settings(_log)`, `period_employee_treatments`; `period_employee_product_sales`; `overtime_settings(_log)`, `overtime_authorizations`, `period_employee_overtime`; `absence_justifications`, `period_employee_absences`; `lateness_settings`, `lateness_tiers`, `lateness_settings_log`, `lateness_justifications`, `period_employee_lateness`; `punctuality_bonus_settings(_log)` (+5 bonus columns on `period_employees`); `attendance_bonus_settings(_log)` (+3 columns); `shift_extension_assignments(_log)` (+3 columns); `tax_parameters(_log)` (`tablas_retencion` gained a unique index, `perceptions` a PK + structured exemption columns); `period_employee_deductions` (linked to `deductions` catalog, +18 ISR columns on `period_employees`); `period_employee_perceptions` (linked to `perceptions` catalog); `period_employee_imss` (frozen IMSS breakdown, +3 columns; SBC lives in `RH.employee_sbc`, see `docs/rh-empleados.md`); `ptu_distributions`, `ptu_distribution_employees`, `ptu_history`, `ptu_distribution_log` (independent of periods); `pending_day_payments`, `period_employee_pending_days` (+`dias_pendientes`/`importe_dias_pendientes`). Catalogs: `tablas_retencion` (keyed by `id_payment_period` + `ejercicio`), `deductions` (1 Seguridad social, 2 ISR), `perceptions` (taxed/exempt engine; row 15 "Días pendientes de pago", `001` fully taxed) and `cat_taxed_exempt` (unused by any calculation).

## Access

- Only `id_role` 1 and 4 (`PAYROLL_ALLOWED_ROLE_IDS` in `lib/payroll/constants.ts`).
- Gated in three places: `proxy.ts` (`/dashboard/nomina` -> `/dashboard`), `navConfig.tsx` (`excludeRoles: [2, 3, 5, 6]`) and `assertPayrollAccess()` in `lib/payroll/access.ts` (server-only; every action returns `{ ok: false }` for other roles). `access.ts` also holds `ActionResult`, `IPayrollSession` and `PERIOD_ROW_SELECT`; not a `"use server"` file, so none of it is a public action.
- Active branch comes from the `sel_sucursal` cookie with JWT fallback, never client input. Each period belongs to a single branch.

## `payroll.periods`

- One row per period per branch: `id_sucursal`, `id_payment_period` (FK to `RH.payment_periods`, SAT `c_PeriodicidadPago`), `codigo`, `ejercicio`, `consecutivo`, four `date` columns (`fecha_inicio`, `fecha_fin`, `fecha_corte`, `fecha_pago`), `status`, `tipo_periodo` (`'O'`/`'E'`), `concepto_extraordinario` (`NULL` in `'O'`, `'A'`/`'P'` in `'E'`, enforced by `CK_periods_concepto_extraordinario`; `ExtraordinaryConcept`), the approval/payment stamp (`approved_by/at`, `paid_by/at`, all `NULL` below status 3), and audit (`created_by/at`, `updated_at`).
- DDL in `queries.txt` (block `NOMINA PAYROLL`). `CK_periods_fechas`: `inicio <= corte <= fin`, `pago >= corte`. `CK_periods_status`: 1..4. `CK_periods_sello_estatus`: ties the stamp to status (`<= 2` none, `3` approval only, `4` approval+payment).
- Types: `interfaces/payroll_period.ts`. Dates are plain strings (see `CLAUDE.md`); SELECTs use `CONVERT(varchar(10|19), ..., 120)`.

## Frequencies

Only SAT keys with a code letter and date rule (`PAYROLL_FREQUENCY_LETTER_BY_SAT_KEY`): 01 D, 02 S, 03 C, 04 Q, 05 M, 06 B, 10 X. 07/08/09 never offered; 99 only exists for the extraordinary period, resolved by `clave_sat` with `status = 0`. Frequency must also be active (`status = 1`) in `RH.payment_periods`, and cannot change after creation.

## Period code

`NOM-{ejercicio}-{letter}{consecutivo, min. 2 digits}`, e.g. `NOM-2026-S38`. `ejercicio` is the year of `fecha_inicio`. `consecutivo` is `MAX + 1` per branch+ejercicio+frequency, computed inside the create batch. Server-generated, read-only, never regenerated. **Gaps are expected, consecutivo never reused** — it is an identifier, not a fiscal folio.

## Suggested dates (`lib/payroll/periodDates.ts`)

`suggestPeriodDates(satKey, previousEndDate, today)` works on `"YYYY-MM-DD"` strings only.

- **Start:** day after last period's end for that frequency/branch; with none: Monday of the current week (weekly), day 1 or 16 (biweekly), day 1 (monthly), today otherwise.
- **End:** weekly = start+6; biweekly = day 15 or last day of month; monthly = last day of month; others = start + fixed length (01: 1, 03: 14, 06: 60, 10: 10).
- **Payment:** last Friday ≤ end. **Cutoff:** payment − 2 days, never before start. Periods shorter than 7 days: payment = end, cutoff = end.
- All four dates always editable; the suggestion is only a starting point.

## Validation and concurrency

- `lib/payroll/schemas.ts` (zod) validates date format, real calendar dates and order. Errors return as `ActionResult`.
- Overlap rejected between periods of the **same frequency in the same branch**; different frequencies may overlap.
- `createPayrollPeriod`/`updatePayrollPeriod` run in one SQL batch with `BEGIN TRAN` and `UPDLOCK, HOLDLOCK` (the explicit transaction keeps the locks held). `UQ_periods_consecutivo` is the last barrier; a race surfaces as "Intenta de nuevo", never a 500. **This same `BEGIN TRAN` + `UPDLOCK, HOLDLOCK` pattern is reused by every status transition below** — not repeated in detail each time.

## Status

`status`: 1 Programada, 2 En cálculo, 3 Aprobada, 4 Pagada (`PAYROLL_PERIOD_STATUS`). Created as 1. 1→2 (calculate) and 2↔2/2→1 (recalculate/revert): see "Salary calculation". 2→3 (approve), 3→4 (pay), 3→2 (unapprove): see "Aprobación y pago". 4→2 (reopen): see "Reapertura". **Pagada (4) is not final**: reopening is the only way out, and it always lands on 2.

- **Edit**: only the four dates + `updated_at`, only at `status = 1` (extraordinary period: only cutoff/payment).
- **Delete**: physical `DELETE`, only at `status = 1`.
- Both re-check `id_sucursal` and `status = 1` inside the statement. At 2 neither edit nor delete; revert to 1 first. At 3/4 same, plus Horas extra/Faltas/Retardos go read-only and the period can't be recalculated/reverted. Reopening from 4 only reaches 2; Editar/Eliminar stay blocked until reverted to 1.
- **Thirteen "Recalcula" checks** (overtime, absences, lateness, both bonus kinds, shift extension, ISR, IMSS, exemption, vacation pay, extraordinary, missing eligible employees, pending day payments) are collected once in `collectRecalculationFlags` (`lib/payroll/recalculationChecks.ts`, server-only), shared by Procesar and the approval lock so they can't disagree. "Missing eligible employees" is the only one also evaluated at status 3. Like ISR/IMSS/exemption/vacation-pay, "pending day payments" only applies to ordinary periods.

## Salary calculation

Screen `/dashboard/nomina/procesar`; actions in `nomina/procesar/actions.ts`.

- **Table `payroll.period_employees`**: one snapshot row per period+employee+type (`tipo_nomina`: `'O'`/`'F'`), with `salario_diario` used, `dias` (net paid days), `dias_falta`, `importe_salario`, `calculated_by/at`. `UQ_period_employees` prevents duplicates and is the index by `id_period`. FK to `periods` has no cascade (a period can only be deleted at status 1, where it has no snapshot).
- **Who's included**: `RH.empleados` with `status = 1`, `activo = 1`, period's branch, `id_periodo_pago` = period's `id_payment_period`, `fecha_ingreso <= fecha_fin`. **No `id_periodo_pago` → never enters payroll**, not even the excluded notice.
- **Days**: `fecha_fin - max(fecha_inicio, fecha_ingreso) + 1` calendar days, **minus `dias_falta`**. `dias` is net; calendar days = `dias + dias_falta`. Lateness is discounted via `dias_retardo`, not via `dias`. `fecha_baja` exists (see `docs/rh-empleados.md`) but isn't used yet — a terminated employee is simply excluded.
- **Amount**: `ROUND(salary * (dias - dias_retardo), 2)` per employee. Operativa uses `salario_diario`, fiscal `salario_diario_fiscal`, **no fallback** between them — an employee can be in one type and not the other.
- **Snapshot** freezes the salary used; editing the employee record later doesn't change a saved result until recalculated.
- **Transitions** (each a single SQL batch with `BEGIN TRAN` + period read `WITH (UPDLOCK, HOLDLOCK)`, branch/status checked inside): `calculatePayrollPeriod` (status 1 or 2 → deletes snapshot, inserts `'O'`/`'F'` rows, leaves at 2 — used for both Calcular and Recalcular); `revertPayrollCalculation` (status 2 → deletes snapshot, returns to 1).
- **Calculation lives in SQL** (`INSERT ... SELECT`); `lib/payroll/salaryCalculation.ts` (`countPaidDays`, `calculateSalaryAmount`) restates it in TS for pure/string use. **If they diverge, SQL is the source of truth** (recurring convention below, not repeated per section).
- **Read side**: `getPayrollProcessPage(filters)` resolves the period (requested → active-today → most recent), returns snapshot rows with puesto/search filters, unfiltered totals, puesto options and "excluded" employees (eligible but without salary of the selected type, computed on the fly).

## Aprobación y pago

Makes statuses 3 (Aprobada) and 4 (Pagada) reachable, with justified disapproval (3→2), a who/when stamp and a log of every change. Actions in `nomina/procesar/actions.ts`; same permissions.

- **Transitions**: `approvePayrollPeriod` (2→3), `payPayrollPeriod` (3→4), `unapprovePayrollPeriod` (3→2, comment required 5–200 chars); a fourth, `reopenPayrollPeriod` (4→2), was added later (see "Reapertura"). Each follows the standard transaction pattern: read period `WITH (UPDLOCK, HOLDLOCK)` checking branch and exact source status, then `UPDATE` + log `INSERT` in the same transaction.
- **Hard lock on approving**: `collectRecalculationFlags` runs **before** opening the transaction; if any of the 13 flags is true, `approvePayrollPeriod` aborts with `PERIOD_NEEDS_RECALCULATION` and writes nothing.
- **Non-blocking warnings** (amber, in the Aprobar modal, computed in `procesar/page.tsx`): ISR/IMSS rows still `'N'` (with count); no calculated employees. The Pagar modal separately warns client-side when today < `fecha_pago`. None block confirming.
- **Stamp**: the four columns on `payroll.periods`, tied by `CK_periods_sello_estatus`. Disapproving clears `approved_by/at` in the same `UPDATE`; history survives in the log.
- **Log `payroll.period_status_log`**: one row per transition, with `status_anterior/nuevo`, `comentario` (required on disapproval and reopening), `changed_by/at`. `CK_period_status_log_transicion` accepts only the (2,3)/(3,4)/(3,2)/(4,2) transitions; `CK_period_status_log_comentario` requires ≥5-char comment when `status_nuevo = 2`. Calculating/reverting (1↔2) are **not** logged here (they have `calculated_at/by` on the snapshot). `ON DELETE CASCADE` to the period (in practice never fires — a period with log rows is never at status 1).
- **Reading**: `lib/payroll/periodStatusLog.ts` (server-only): `loadPeriodStatusStamp(idPeriod)`, `loadPeriodStatusLog(idPeriod, idSucursal)` (newest first, branch-scoped). `getPayrollProcessPage` loads the stamp only at status 3/4; the log is its own Server Component.
- **Freezing**: Horas extra/Faltas/Retardos already reject writes at status ≥3 ("El periodo ya no admite cambios", `validateJustificationTarget`) and hide "Acciones" (`canDecide`); Periodos restricts Editar/Eliminar to status 1; `findPtuDistributionPayrollUsage` blocks "Reabrir" a PTU distribution with `p.status >= 2`. All of these unfreeze automatically when a paid period reopens to 2 (they're whitelists of `{1, 2}`).
- **UI** (`/dashboard/nomina/procesar`): Aprobar / Marcar como pagada / Desaprobar buttons extend `PayrollCalculationActions` (`ACTIONS_BY_STATUS`: 2→approve, 3→unapprove then pay, 4→reopen), each with its own in-UI confirmation (no native `confirm()`); Aprobar disables with `aria-describedby` when `recalculationNeeded`. Desaprobar's dialog has a required `<textarea>` with char counter. `PayrollPeriodStatusStamp` (Server Component) shows who/when approved and, at status 4, paid; hidden without a stamp. `PayrollPeriodStatusLog` is a `<details>` "Historial de estatus", `null` with no rows.
- **Not built**: per-type/per-employee approval, bulk approval/payment, capturing real payment date/reference/method, two-signature flow, notifications, locking `setShiftExtensionAssignment` on an approved period's days. **Known risk**: retroactive captures (vacations, economic days, treatment payments) on an approved/paid period's days still revert nothing and are unrecalculable — documented, not fixed.

## Reapertura de un periodo pagado

Adds `reopenPayrollPeriod` (4→2), so an employee omitted from an already-closed period (hired/reactivated/transferred in, or `id_periodo_pago` captured late) can be detected and paid — nothing else catches this, since the employee has no snapshot row and doesn't appear in `ExcludedEmployeesNotice` (which only lists people with a `NULL`/`≤0` salary for the type). Same action file/permissions.

- **Transition**: `status 4 → 2`, clearing all four stamp columns (already allowed by `CK_periods_sello_estatus`'s `<= 2` branch). Comment required 5–200 chars, shares `periodStatusCommentSchema`/`STATUS_COMMENT_MIN/MAX_LENGTH` with `unapprovePayrollPeriod`. Standard transaction pattern; marker `PERIOD_NOT_REOPENABLE`. The pay-once locks on treatments/sales/overtime/vacations (per-period `NOT EXISTS`) mean recalculating a reopened period can't duplicate a payment or steal one from a later period.
- Only `CK_period_status_log_transicion` changed in the database (dropped/recreated adding `(4, 2)`); no new table/column — the missing-employee notice is computed live.
- **Detection: the twelfth check, `missingEligibleEmployees`**. `loadMissingEligibleEmployees` (`lib/payroll/missingEmployeesRecalculation.ts`, server-only) finds employees eligible **today** with salary `> 0` for the type but **no row** in `period_employees` — reusing the same eligibility constants `calculatePayrollPeriod`'s `INSERT` uses. Covers ordinary and Christmas-bonus periods; **a PTU period always returns `[]`** (its universe is the closed distribution, not `RH.empleados`). Only ever flags someone **missing**, never someone no longer eligible.
- **Lives at status 2 and 3** (unlike the other 12, which only fire at 2) — at 3 there's still a cheap way out (Desaprobar). Enters `collectRecalculationFlags`, so Aprobar is blocked with no new code.
- **New hard lock on paying**: `payPayrollPeriod` calls `loadMissingEligibleEmployees` before opening its transaction and aborts with `PERIOD_HAS_MISSING_EMPLOYEES_MESSAGE` if it returns rows. Only way out at status 3: Desaprobar, Recalcular, Aprobar.
- **UI**: `MissingEmployeesNotice` (Server Component) mirrors `ExcludedEmployeesNotice`'s shape, grouped by `tipo_nomina`. `getPayrollProcessPage` always returns both types' missing employees regardless of URL type. `ACTIONS_BY_STATUS[4] = ["reopen"]` with the same destructive `<textarea>` pattern as Desaprobar; Marcar como pagada disables with `aria-describedby` when `hasMissingEmployees`.
- **Known risk**: the twelfth check doesn't run at status 4, so an omission on an already-paid period is invisible on screen. Mitigation: a hand-run diagnostic query in `queries.txt` lists every eligible-but-missing employee across periods at status 3/4; the fix is Reabrir.

## Días pendientes de pago

Hand capture of a day already worked but never paid (fell outside any period's range, or was discounted by mistake), settled in an ordinary period's calculation at its own frozen rate per payroll.

- **Why**: `calculatePayrollPeriod` only looks inside `[fecha_inicio, fecha_fin]`; a day before that range has no way into either payroll. (Reabrir fixes a whole missed period; this fixes a handful of days for one person without touching anything already dispersed.) Not a justified absence (discounts the *current* period) and not an economic/vacation day — neither mechanism pays a *past* period's day.
- **Screen** `nomina/dias-pendientes/` (roles 1/4, same three locks, branch from `sel_sucursal`), state in URL (`periodo`, `q`, `pagina`), reusing `PayrollPeriodFilterToolbar`/`PayrollPagerFooter`/`PayrollEmptyState`. **Period selector only offers ordinary periods** — the one selected pays the capture. Table: employee, date, both rates, each payroll's estimated amount, reason, author, capture date, "Pagado en {código}" badge once paid. Summary cards cover the whole period.
- **Capture by range, one modal** (`PendingDayPaymentModal`): employee, start/end date (one row per day, inclusive), daily operativa/fiscal rates preloaded from current `salario_diario`/`salario_diario_fiscal` and editable, required reason (5–200 chars). **A rate at 0 = that payroll doesn't pay the day**; both at 0 rejected. Rejected: dates after the paying period's `fecha_fin`, before `fecha_ingreso`, or already captured for that employee. Non-blocking amber notices: a date inside an already-calculated period (status 2/3/4 — exactly the case this fixes); an employee with no salary of a type.
- **Delete** (physical, in-UI confirmation) while status 1/2; no edit, only delete-and-recapture. Capture and delete both go through `validateJustificationTarget` (read-only at status 3/4). Fixing something already paid: Reabrir or capture in the next period.
- **Data model**. `payroll.pending_day_payments` (the capture: `id_period` — the paying period — `id_empleado`, `fecha`, both rates, `motivo`, author, timestamp; `UNIQUE (id_empleado, fecha)` so a day is owed only once ever) and `payroll.period_employee_pending_days` (paid breakdown, one row per day+`tipo_nomina`, rate/amount/reason frozen; `UNIQUE (id_empleado, fecha, tipo_nomina)` — a day paid once per type across every period, frequency, branch; no FK to the capture). `period_employees` gains `dias_pendientes`/`importe_dias_pendientes` per type.
- **Calculation** (`calculatePayrollPeriod`, ordinary only, right after `#vacation_pay`). Shared SQL in `lib/payroll/pendingDaysSql.ts`, used by both the calculation and the Recalcula check. `#pending_days` expands captures into one row per capture+type, dropping a 0 rate and any day another period already paid in that type. Amount per day = `ROUND(tarifa_diaria, 2)`. **A capture whose employee has no row of that type is not paid and not locked** — fixable later by capturing the salary and recalculating. `revertPayrollCalculation` unchanged: the cascade clears the breakdown, captures survive.
- **Fiscal**: one more `UNION ALL` in `#fiscal_perception_lines` for perception 15 "Días pendientes de pago" (SAT `001`, fully taxed), only when amount `> 0`. **`pe.dias` is untouched** — a retroactive day is sueldo for CFDI purposes but doesn't move the subsidy, per-day caps or IMSS days.
- **Thirteenth "Recalcula" check**, `pendingDaysRecalculationNeeded` (status 2, ordinary only): fires when a capture went unpaid in every type, a breakdown row's capture no longer exists, or its frozen rate differs from today's.
- **"Capturas que no se pagarán" notice** (`PendingDaysNotCoveredNotice`, shared by Procesar and this screen): lists, by employee/type, a capture `> 0` whose employee has no row of that type in `period_employees` — checks the snapshot, not the current salary (only capturing **and recalculating** clears it). Purely informational.
- **Rest-of-module UI**: Procesar "Días pendientes" column; Detalle `dias_pendientes` line in `buildPerceptionLines` plus `PayrollPendingDaysList`; `navConfig.tsx` gains "Días pendientes". None of this appears in an extraordinary period.
- **Not built**: two-step authorize flow (capture already is authorization — only roles 1/4 reach the module); editing a capture (delete-and-recapture); log of deleted captures; automatic detection of unpaid days; negative days/manual deductions; paying a terminated employee; receipts/CFDI/dispersion; caps per capture.
- **Known risks**: nothing stops capturing as "pending" a day the normal calculation already paid in its own range — would double-pay. Mitigation: amber notice + "Pagado en {código}" badge (capturist's judgment call, not a system error — blocking it would close the case this spec exists for). Separately, a hand-run diagnostic query lists any capture of a period at status 3/4 with no row in `period_employee_pending_days` (a day captured but never paid — should be prevented by the thirteenth check; a hit means that check was bypassed or the period changed status mid-capture).

## Comisión por consultas

Screen `/dashboard/nomina/comisiones` (Server Component; client leaves: `CommissionTierModal`, `DeleteCommissionTierButton`); actions in `nomina/comisiones/actions.ts` (`getCommissionTiers`, `createCommissionTier`, `updateCommissionTier`, `deleteCommissionTier`), behind `assertPayrollAccess()`, scoped to the session's `id_empresa`.

- **Catalog `payroll.commission_tiers`**: per company, `min_consultas`, `max_consultas` (`NULL` = open tier), `importe` (fixed per tier, not per consulta). Seed: 1-10→$100, 11-25→$200, 26-35→$300, 36+→$500. Physical delete. **Live catalog**: editing never changes a saved snapshot until recalculated.
- **Tier rules** (`commissionTierSchema` + re-validated in SQL in the write transaction): `min >= 1`, `max >= min` or null, `importe >= 0`, **no overlapping ranges**, **at most one open tier**. **Gaps allowed** (a count in a gap earns $0). Modal pre-warns with `tierRangeOverlaps`; server is authority.
- **Which consultas count**: `consultas.id_podologo` is one of the `users.id_user` linked to the employee (`users.id_empleado`), `deleted_at IS NULL`, not cancelled, `fecha_fin IS NOT NULL`, `fecha` inside the period. `cancelada` is nullable, `NULL` = not cancelled (~98% of rows), so SQL uses `ISNULL(cancelada, 0) = 0` — a plain `= 0` would count nothing.
- **No branch filter, no user `status` filter** (both apply across specs 56–58 below too, not repeated).
- **One tier per employee**: counts of all linked users are summed; a single tier applies to the total, paying its full `importe` (not prorated).
- **Only operativa (`'O'`)**; `'F'` rows get 0/0. An employee without operativa salary doesn't enter payroll, so no commission.
- **Snapshot**: `period_employees` gains `consultas_atendidas`, `importe_comision`, filled by `calculatePayrollPeriod`'s `INSERT ... SELECT` via two `OUTER APPLY`. `lib/payroll/commissionTiers.ts` mirrors the rule in TS.
- **Screens**: Procesar "Com. consultas" column; Detalle `comision_consultas` line (omitted at 0, shows the tier range only if it still matches today's catalog).
- **Out of scope**: tiers per branch/role, effective dates, list of counted consultas, fiscal commission, manual adjustment.

## Comisión por tratamientos de onicomicosis

Pays a fixed amount per onychomycosis treatment registered by an employee's linked users and **settled** inside the period. Rules of origin: `references/docs/tratamiento_onicomicosis.md`. Configuration on `/dashboard/nomina/comisiones` (below the tiers table); calculation in `calculatePayrollPeriod`.

- **Settlement rule**: a treatment is settled on the `created_at` of the partial payment (`id_tratamiento_pago_tipo = 2`, `status = 1`) at which the running sum of such payments (ordered by `created_at` then `id_tratamiento_pago`) reaches `umbral_liquidacion` (`>=`, seed $5,000). Type 1 (advance) and `status = 0` payments never count. **The settlement date decides the period**, not the treatment's `created_at`.
- **Attribution**: `Tratamiento_onicomicosis.id_usuario` → `users.id_empleado` (not `id_especialista`, not the consulta's `id_podologo`). Same linked-user/no-branch/no-status rules as spec 56.
- **What counts**: `ISNULL(id_stage, 0) <> 6` (not cancelled) at calculation time; settlement date in range; not already in `period_employee_treatments`.
- **Amount**: `tratamientos × importe_por_tratamiento` (seed $500), no proration/cap. Operativa only.
- **Configuration + log**: `payroll.treatment_commission_settings` (PK `id_empresa`): `importe_por_tratamiento` (0 = off) and `umbral_liquidacion` (>0), audit. No row = nobody earns it. `treatment_commission_settings_log` logs only real changes. `updateTreatmentCommissionSettings` (`zod` + transaction with `UPDLOCK, HOLDLOCK`). Actions in `nomina/comisiones/actions.ts`; only `TreatmentCommissionSettingsModal` is client.
- **Pay-once lock**: `period_employee_treatments`, `UNIQUE (id_tratamiento)` — paid once in all history, whatever period/frequency/branch. FK to `period_employees` `ON DELETE CASCADE`; no FK to `Tratamiento_onicomicosis`. Two competing calculations → one wins, the other gets "Otro cálculo tomó alguno de estos tratamientos al mismo tiempo. Intenta de nuevo."
- **Order in the batch**: delete snapshot (cascade frees treatments) → build `#liquidated` (cumulative `SUM() OVER`) → insert snapshot with the three new columns → insert breakdown from the same set. `lib/payroll/treatmentCommission.ts` mirrors it in TS.
- **Consequences**: an employee not in that period's operativa payroll never gets paid for a treatment, and it's never marked paid (so it never commissions later either — fix the employee and recalculate that period). Changing the threshold recomputes settlement dates of unpaid treatments on the next calculation. **No retroactive discount**: cancelling/deleting after a period paid it changes nothing unless that same period is recalculated. Snapshots from before this spec stay at 0 until recalculated.
- **Screens**: Procesar "Com. onicomicosis"; Detalle `comision_tratamientos` line + `PayrollPaidTreatmentsList` (patient, settlement date, link to the treatment).
- **Out of scope**: reversing a paid commission, commission for specialist/podólogo, fiscal payroll, per-branch/role/type amounts, effective dates, type-1 payments toward the threshold, manual adjustment, releasing a paid treatment by hand, a global paid/pending report.

## Comisión por venta de productos

Pays `bono_venta` for every piece an employee's linked users sold inside the period (consultas or direct sales). Each line pays `cantidad × bono_venta`. Rules of origin: `references/docs/comision_ventas.md` (differs in one point: for consultas the period is decided by `consultas.fecha`, not `created_at`, to match spec 56). Calculation in `calculatePayrollPeriod`.

- **Sources/attribution**: consulta lines via `consulta_productos`, attributed to the consulta's `id_podologo`; direct sales via `VentasDetalle`, attributed to `Ventas.id_usuario` (whoever captured it — a shared cashier account linked to an employee gives that employee every sale made with it). Both through `users.id_empleado`, same no-branch/no-status rules.
- **What counts**: consulta line needs `consulta_productos.status = 1`, consulta `deleted_at IS NULL` and `ISNULL(cancelada,0)=0`, `consultas.fecha` in range (`fecha_fin IS NOT NULL` **not** required, unlike spec 56, since the product sale/stock discount already happened); direct sale needs `Ventas.status = 1`, `created_at` in range. Both: `bono_venta > 0` (lines at 0/NULL aren't locked, so a later-captured bonus can still be paid by recalculating); line not already in `period_employee_product_sales`; `Products.status` ignored.
- **Amount**: `ROUND(cantidad × bono_venta, 2)` per line, summed per employee. Bonus frozen at calculation time (current catalog value). For `split = 1` products, paid per piece. Operativa only.
- **Snapshot**: `piezas_vendidas` (`SUM(cantidad)`), `importe_comision_productos`, both `CHECK >= 0`.
- **Pay-once lock**: `period_employee_product_sales`, key `(origen 'C'/'V', id_linea_origen)` instead of two nullable FKs (a filtered unique index would need per-connection `SET` options) — `UNIQUE (origen, id_linea_origen)`, paid once ever. `ON DELETE CASCADE`; no FK to `consulta_productos`/`VentasDetalle`/`Products` (deletes must survive there). Competing calculations → "Otro cálculo tomó algunas de estas ventas al mismo tiempo. Intenta de nuevo."
- **Order in batch**: after `#liquidated` (spec 57) — build `#product_sales` (consulta ∪ direct-sale lines) → insert snapshot columns via `OUTER APPLY` (only `'O'`) → insert breakdown. `lib/payroll/productSalesCommission.ts` mirrors it.
- **Consequences**: not-in-payroll sellers get neither pay nor the paid mark. Bonus changes don't affect already-paid lines. The same consulta can yield both the consultas commission and a product commission. **No retroactive discount.**
- **Screens**: Procesar "Com. Ventas" (amount + piece count); Detalle `comision_productos` line + `PayrollSoldProductsList` (grouped by product and frozen bonus).
- **Out of scope**: reversing a paid commission, fiscal payroll, historical/validity bonus dates, per-branch/role or percentage bonus, attributing to whoever added the product, a global report, editing the bonus from payroll (edited in the product catalog).
- **Known risks**: legacy `consulta_productos` rows from before a past migration store an old `dbo.productos` id in `id_producto` — don't create periods with dates before that migration. If the calculation gets slow, index `consultas([fecha])` and `Ventas([created_at])`.

## Horas extra (detección y autorización)

Detects overtime per employee/day from checadas; an admin authorizes (adjusting hours), rejects, or leaves pending. Rules of origin: `references/docs/horas_extras.md`. Payment is a separate step (below); the screen notes "Las horas autorizadas se pagan al calcular la nómina del periodo."

- **Screen/actions**: `/dashboard/nomina/horas-extra`, state in URL (`periodo`, `estado=pendiente|autorizada|rechazada`, `q`, `pagina`; 25/page). `actions.ts`: `getOvertimePage`, `getOvertimeSettings`, `getOvertimeSettingsLog`, `updateOvertimeSettings`, `decideOvertimeDay`, `clearOvertimeDecision`, all behind `assertPayrollAccess()`. Types: `interfaces/payroll_overtime.ts`.
- **Who's reviewed**: employees eligible for the operativa payroll (`ELIGIBLE_OPERATIVE_EMPLOYEE_CONDITIONS` in `lib/payroll/eligibleEmployees.ts`: status/activo/branch/frequency, `salario_diario > 0`, `fecha_ingreso <= fecha_fin`). Only days from `max(fecha_inicio, fecha_ingreso)` to `fecha_fin`. `ELIGIBLE_EMPLOYEE_BASE_CONDITIONS` is the same minus the salary clause — reused by the overtime-payment spec. `resolvePeriod` (`lib/payroll/period.ts`) is shared with Procesar.
- **Detection** (`lib/payroll/overtimeDetection.ts`, pure, strings only): checadas grouped by day of `fecha_hora`, first `entrada` + last `salida` used (lunch in between doesn't count). A day is **incomplete** with the same criterion as the attendance tab (`summarizeAttendanceEvents`). Scheduled day: extra = `max(0, hora_entrada_1 - first entrada)` + `max(0, last salida - end of last block)` (late arrival **not** deducted). Rest day (no schedule row): every hour is extra. Rounded **down** to 30-min blocks, no minimum. No schedule rows at all → never detects anything ("Sin horario definido" notice). **Current** schedule used for the whole period (no history). Weekday from `Date.UTC`; times subtracted as integer minutes.
- **Tables**: `overtime_settings` (one row/company: `limite_horas_dobles_periodo` seed 9, `tope_horas_dia` seed 16 — the limit is consumed by the payment spec); `overtime_settings_log`; `overtime_authorizations` (`UNIQUE (id_empleado, fecha)`, `estado 'A'/'R'`, `horas_autorizadas`, `horas_detectadas` at decision time, optional comment, who/when — **no row = Pendiente**, "Volver a pendiente" deletes the row, deciding again overwrites it — no history). `CK_overtime_authorizations_estado` needs explicit `IS NOT NULL` on hours in the `'A'` branch (else `NULL > 0` is UNKNOWN and passes).
- **The decision belongs to the employee's day, not the period** — no `id_period` FK, so it survives period deletion/recreation. Writes validate inside the transaction that the period is the active branch, status 1/2, `fecha` in range, employee eligible that day.
- **Decisions** (`decideOvertimeDay`): authorized hours multiple of 0.5, `>0`, `<= tope_horas_dia` (read live, not a `CHECK`, since the cap can change without affecting saved authorizations). Can exceed detected hours (covers missing checadas). `horas_detectadas` always server-recomputed. Rejecting stores `NULL` hours. Read-only at status 3/4.
- **List**: shown if detected overtime, incomplete, or has a saved decision. If detection today differs from `horas_detectadas` at decision time: "Detectado cambió: X -> Y", keeps `horas_autorizadas`. Summary cards cover the whole period.
- **Components**: Server `OvertimeDaysTable`, `OvertimeSummaryCards`, `EmployeesWithoutScheduleNotice`, `OvertimeSettingsCard`, `OvertimeSettingsLog`; client only `OvertimeToolbar`, `OvertimeSettingsModal`, `OvertimeDecisionModal` (portaled to `document.body` to escape a `text-right` table cell).
- **Out of scope**: fiscal payroll/ISR, tolerances/attendance discounts, prima dominical/holidays, midnight-crossing shifts, schedule history, bulk authorize, adding unlisted days.
- **Known risks**: a checador sending a wrong `tipo` produces bulk incomplete days (listed, not lost). A midnight-crossing shift leaves one incomplete day and a stray `salida`.

## Horas extra: pago

`calculatePayrollPeriod` pays authorized overtime. Rules of origin: `references/docs/horas_extras.md`. Paid in **both** payrolls (same authorizations and split, at `salario_diario` in `'O'` and `salario_diario_fiscal` in `'F'`).

- **What's paid**: only `estado = 'A'` rows inside the period, on/after `fecha_ingreso`, for operativa-eligible employees, of a company **with** an `overtime_settings` row (without one: nothing paid, no silent default of 9). The calculation never re-reads checadas — no decision, no pay.
- **Split**: per employee, chronological order, first `limite_horas_dobles_periodo` hours double, rest triple (running sum via `SUM() OVER`); the day crossing the limit splits. Limit 0 → everything triple. Days already paid by another period are excluded **before** the running sum.
- **Rate/rounding**: hourly rate = `salario_diario / 8` of the frozen type, ×2 or ×3. Each day rounded on its own (`ROUND(horas × rate × multiplier / 8, 2)`); intermediate rate never rounded. Snapshot sums already-rounded daily amounts.
- **Tables**: `period_employees` gains `horas_extra_dobles/triples`, `importe_horas_extra_dobles/triples`, `limite_horas_dobles_aplicado` (`CK_period_employees_horas_extra`: `dobles <= limite_aplicado`); `period_employee_overtime` (one row/paid day, frozen split+amounts, `ON DELETE CASCADE`, **no FK to `overtime_authorizations`** so "Volver a pendiente" never collides).
- **Pay-once lock**: `UQ_period_employee_overtime_empleado_fecha_tipo` = `UNIQUE (id_empleado, fecha, tipo_nomina)` — paid once per type across all history. Not keyed by authorization id (deleting/re-authorizing changes that id). Competing calculations → "Otro cálculo tomó algunas de estas horas extra al mismo tiempo. Intenta de nuevo."
- **In the batch**: `#overtime_days` built after `#product_sales`, carries `tipo_nomina` (crossed with both salary types, a 0-salary type generates no rows); snapshot and breakdown insert from the same temp table. `lib/payroll/overtimePay.ts` mirrors the rule.
- **"Recalcula" notice**: `isOvertimeRecalculationNeeded` (status 2 only): an authorized day unpaid in that type; a breakdown day no longer authorized or with different hours; or a current limit different from the applied one. Shown via shared `PayrollRecalculationNotice.tsx` in Procesar and Horas extra.
- **Screens**: Procesar "Horas extra" column; Detalle `horas_extra_dobles/triples` lines + `PayrollOvertimeDaysList` (also shown in fiscal); Horas extra shows "Pagada en {código}" badge per day, decision still changeable at status 1/2.
- **Consequences**: a paid day can still be rejected afterward — nothing changes until recalculated (no retroactive discount in approved/paid periods — must be blocked there). A frequency change can split the same date across two periods, each with its own double-hours accounting. **Non-weekly fiscal periods** still pay the amount, but the double perception and ISR stay unsupported/`'N'`.
- **Out of scope**: CFDI `HorasExtra` node, simple hours/minimum-wage exemption, a separate fiscal double limit, triple-hour cap, per-week limit, configurable multipliers, auto-recalculation on authorization change, releasing a paid day by hand, prima dominical, lateness/absence discounts here.
- **Known risks**: this concept chains onto salary+3 commissions in `calculatePayrollPeriod` — any change to its `INSERT`/`WHERE` must be verified by diffing an already-calculated period's snapshot before/after.

## Faltas

Screen `/dashboard/nomina/faltas`; actions in `nomina/faltas/actions.ts`. Rules of origin: `references/docs/faltas.md` §3.1. Only absences (lateness is its own section below).

- **Who's controlled**: employee in **any** payroll of the period (`ELIGIBLE_ANY_PAYROLL_EMPLOYEE_CONDITIONS`) **and** has a linked, active (`status=1`) user with `id_role` 2 (podólogo; `ABSENCE_CONTROL_ROLE_IDS` in `lib/payroll/constants.ts`), evaluated **at the moment**, no history (`ABSENCE_CONTROLLED_EMPLOYEE_CONDITION`, one `EXISTS` shared across screen/writes/calculation/notice). Everyone else keeps `dias_falta = 0` with no warning. **A podólogo without a linked active user is never controlled** — check the Usuarios tab first.
- **What's an absence**: a day in `max(fecha_inicio, fecha_ingreso)`..`fecha_fin`, strictly before today, with a schedule row for its weekday and **no checada at all** (an incomplete punch counts as attendance — that's Horas extra's business). Current schedule used for the whole period. No schedule → no absences generated, flagged in "Sin horario definido". Today from `addZeroToday(new Date())`, never `GETDATE()`.
- **Justification**: `payroll.absence_justifications` (`UNIQUE (id_empleado, fecha)`, no `id_period`): `'J'` justified (PDF/JPG/PNG ≤5MB, Cloudinary URL via `/api/upload`); `'N'` "No aplica" with mandatory comment (holidays, offline checador; vacation days auto-excluded). **No row = unjustified.** No approval step. `CK_absence_justifications_estado` enforces the shape per state.
- **Writes** (`justifyAbsence`, `markAbsenceNotApplicable`, `clearAbsenceJustification`): `assertPayrollAccess`, zod, one transaction reading the period `WITH (UPDLOCK, HOLDLOCK)`. Shared with Retardos: `validateJustificationTarget`, `upsertJustification` (`lib/payroll/justificationWrites.ts`) re-check branch, status 1/2, date range, controlled employee, `fecha >= fecha_ingreso`; justify/no-aplica also require date < today, a schedule that weekday, no checada. `clearAbsenceJustification` skips the day checks. Read-only at status 3/4.
- **Calculation**: `calculatePayrollPeriod` builds `#absences` (recursive `period_days` CTE, `OPTION (MAXRECURSION 400)`), minus justified days. `OUTER APPLY` counts, per employee+type, absences **not already discounted by another period for that type**; `dias_falta` gets the count, `dias` = net paid days, `importe_salario = ROUND(salario × dias, 2)`. Applies to `'O'` **and** `'F'`. Snapshots from before this spec default to 0.
- **Breakdown/lock**: `period_employee_absences`, `UNIQUE (id_empleado, fecha, tipo_nomina)` — discounted once per type, whatever the period. `ON DELETE CASCADE`; no FK to `absence_justifications`. Competing calculations → "Otro cálculo tomó algunas de estas faltas al mismo tiempo. Intenta de nuevo."
- **Source of truth**: `lib/payroll/absenceDetection.ts` (`detectEmployeeAbsences`, `formatIsoWeekday`, pure). ISO weekday in SQL: `(DATEDIFF(day, '19000101', fecha) % 7) + 1` (independent of `DATEFIRST`).
- **"Recalcula" notice**: `isAbsenceRecalculationNeeded` (status 2 only) compares today's would-be-discounted set with the breakdown: a new absence undiscounted, or a discounted day no longer an absence (justified, new checada, schedule change, employee lost the role). "Volver a injustificada" leaves no trace, hence comparing live sets rather than timestamps.
- **Screens**: Faltas (state in URL `periodo`, `estado=injustificada|justificada|no_aplica|vacaciones`, `q`, `pagina`, 25/page); rows show "Descontada en {código}"; shared justification components (`JustificationUploadModal`, `NotApplicableModal`, `ClearJustificationButton`, generic since Retardos reuses them). Procesar: "F faltas" below "Días". Detalle: `sueldo_base` line reads days breakdown; `PayrollAbsencesList`.
- **Consequences**: **no retroactive discount** in approved/paid periods (must be blocked there, still isn't). A dead checador makes every controlled employee look absent that day — review before "Calcular", mark "No aplica". No holiday catalog. Cloudinary files aren't deleted on replace.
- **Out of scope**: lateness (own section), bonuses, holiday/vacation/sick-leave catalogs, second-user approval, per-employee config, schedule history, bulk justification, manual undetected-absence entry, deductions as their own line item.
- **Known risks**: chains onto the calculate batch — verify by diffing snapshots. If slow, index `RH.asistencias(id_empleado, fecha_hora)`.

## Retardos

Screen `/dashboard/nomina/retardos`; actions in `nomina/retardos/actions.ts`. Rules of origin: `references/docs/faltas.md` §3.2. Sibling of Faltas, reuses most of it. Only arrival lateness against `hora_entrada_1`.

- **Who's controlled**: same population as Faltas (no separate constant — split only if the rules diverge). Same caveat about unlinked podólogos.
- **What's lateness**: a day in `max(fecha_inicio, fecha_ingreso)`..`min(fecha_fin, today)` (**today included** — final as soon as the entry punch exists), with a schedule row, at least one `entrada` punch (the **first** is the arrival) and `minutos = floor((arrival - hora_entrada_1)/60s)` **strictly greater than** `tolerancia_minutos`. Seconds truncated. Only `hora_entrada_1` compared. A day with only `salida` isn't evaluated; a day with no punches is Faltas' business — never both. Current schedule for the whole period; rest days never evaluated.
- **Classification**: `minutos >= minutos_retardo_grave` = **severe** (`'G'`), else **accumulable** (`'A'`) — mutually exclusive.
- **Configuration + log**: `payroll.lateness_settings` (PK `id_empresa`): `tolerancia_minutos` (seed 10), `minutos_retardo_grave` (seed 30), `dias_descuento_retardo_grave` (0.5/1, seed 0.5). `lateness_tiers` per company+frequency (`retardos`, `dias_descuento`; seeds: weekly {1→0.5, 2→1}, biweekly {2→0.5, 4→1}). `lateness_settings_log` (tiers as JSON, since count varies). **No settings row → discounts nothing**; no tiers for a frequency → only severe delays discount, "Sin escalones para {frecuencia}". `updateLatenessSettings`: transaction with `UPDLOCK, HOLDLOCK`, validates tolerance positive, severe threshold > tolerance, severe discount 0.5/1, tiers no repeats, `dias_descuento` multiple of 0.5 and **not decreasing** as `retardos` grows (can't be a `CHECK` — cross-row). Saving without changes writes nothing; `updated_at` moves on any change (tiers included), since the Recalcula notice depends on it.
- **Discount**, per employee+type, counting only unjustified delays not already discounted elsewhere for that type: severe = `G × dias_descuento_retardo_grave`; accumulable through tiers **in cycles** (`ciclo = MAX(retardos)`: `floor(A/ciclo) × top-tier discount + discount of the highest tier with retardos <= A mod ciclo`). `dias_retardo = min(uncapped, dias)` (salary never negative); `importe_salario = ROUND(salario × (dias - dias_retardo), 2)`, in `'O'` and `'F'`. `period_employees` gains `dias_retardo decimal(4,1)`, `dias_retardo_sin_tope decimal(5,1)`. **`dias` keeps its meaning** (net days after absences).
- **Justification**: `payroll.lateness_justifications`, same shape as absences, own Cloudinary folder `clinica/empleados/retardos`. Sibling table on purpose (a type column would let a punch arriving on an absence-justified day be misread as lateness justification).
- **Writes**: same pattern as Faltas, via shared `validateJustificationTarget`; justify/no-aplica also require date ≤ today, a schedule, an `entrada` punch and that it's at least `tolerancia_minutos` late (`validateDayIsLateness`, current tolerance).
- **Calculation**: `#lateness` built after `#absences` with a `cuenta_para_descuento` bit (severe always counts; accumulable only if the company has tiers for the frequency — so a tierless frequency neither discounts nor locks those days for another period). Discount `OUTER APPLY` and `period_employee_lateness` insert filter by that bit; the punctuality bonus reads the unfiltered list. Minutes truncated via `CONVERT(varchar(8), llegada, 108)` + `DATEDIFF(second,...)/60`.
- **Breakdown/lock**: `period_employee_lateness`, `UNIQUE (id_empleado, fecha, tipo_nomina)`, frozen `hora_entrada_1`, `hora_llegada`, `minutos_retardo`, `clasificacion`. `ON DELETE CASCADE`, no FK to justifications.
- **Source of truth**: `lib/payroll/latenessDetection.ts` (pure).
- **"Recalcula" notice**: `isLatenessRecalculationNeeded` (status 2): a delay of today unpaid, a discounted delay no longer one or reclassified, or `lateness_settings.updated_at` later than `MIN(calculated_at)`.
- **Screens**: Retardos (`periodo`, `estado=injustificado|justificado|no_aplica`, `tipo=grave|acumulable`, `q`, `pagina`, 25/page); settings card + modal + log above the list (editable anytime, independent of justification locks). Procesar: "R retardo" next to "F faltas". Detalle: `sueldo_base` days breakdown + `PayrollLatenessList`.
- **Consequences**: no retroactive discount in approved/paid periods. A skewed-clock checador produces bulk false delays; a wrong-`tipo` checador can turn a lunch return into a false severe delay — review before calculating. Extreme tiers can zero the base salary (mitigated by the cap + log).
- **Out of scope**: `hora_entrada_2` return and early departures, the attendance bonus (own section; punctuality bonus too), delays on rest days, per-branch/employee config, second-user approval, bulk justification, schedule history, retroactive discount in 3/4, deleting Cloudinary files, a holiday catalog.
- **Known risks**: chains onto salary+commissions+overtime+absences — verify by diffing snapshots (e.g. deleting `lateness_settings` in a rolled-back transaction proves a tierless period stays identical). Index `RH.asistencias(id_empleado, fecha_hora)` if slow.

## Bono de puntualidad

Screen `/dashboard/nomina/bonos`; actions in `nomina/bonos/actions.ts`. Rules of origin: `references/docs/puntualidad.md`, `references/nomina/reglas_modulo_nomina.md` §2.2. A podólogo present the whole period earns a configurable bonus, lost when unjustified delays + absences exceed the configured maximum. (Attendance bonus is a sibling tab, below; settings/modal/log components are generic and shared by both.)

- **Who's evaluated**: the operativa row of the same controlled population as Faltas/Retardos, only if: the company has a `lateness_settings` row, the frequency has an active `punctuality_bonus_settings` row, `fecha_ingreso <= fecha_inicio` (no proration), at least one schedule row. Otherwise **"No evaluado"** with a reason (`no_lateness_settings`, `bonus_not_configured`, `joined_mid_period`, `no_schedule`, in that order), amount 0. `'F'` rows always "No evaluado".
- **Incidents** = unjustified delays (up to today) + unjustified absences (up to yesterday), spec-62/63 detection rules. Severe or accumulable each count 1. Justified (`'J'`/`'N'`) days don't count; **accumulable delays of a tierless frequency, and days another period already discounted, do** count (the discount lock avoids double-discounting, not whether it happened).
- **Result**: `incidents <= maximo_incidencias` keeps the whole `monto`, else $0. No proration. **Same tolerance as Retardos** — no tolerance of its own.
- **Configuration + log**: `punctuality_bonus_settings` (PK `id_empresa`+`id_payment_period`): `monto`, `maximo_incidencias`, `status`, audit; `CK_punctuality_bonus_settings_valores` requires `monto > 0`, `maximo_incidencias >= 0`. Seeds: company 1, weekly+biweekly, $500/max 1/active. No row or `status=0` → no bonus. `updatePunctualityBonusSettings`: transaction with `UPDLOCK, HOLDLOCK`, upserts **only changed frequencies**, logs each. Saving without changes writes nothing.
- **Snapshot**: `bono_puntualidad_resultado` (`'C'`/`'P'`/`'N'`), `_retardos`, `_faltas`, `_maximo` (NULL if `'N'`), `importe_bono_puntualidad`. `CK_period_employees_bono_puntualidad` ties the result to the numbers and to `tipo_nomina='O'`. No breakdown table/lock — bonus paid once per `'O'` row; the underlying delays/absences are visible in their own screens.
- **Calculation**: in SQL inside the batch (`lib/payroll/punctualityBonusSql.ts`: `PUNCTUALITY_BONUS_APPLY_SQL`, `PUNCTUALITY_BONUS_COLUMNS`), shared with the notice. **Calculating before the period ends pays with partial data** (absences only count to yesterday) — recalculate after `fecha_fin`, before approving.
- **Source of truth**: `lib/payroll/punctualityBonus.ts` (doesn't distinguish the "No evaluado" reason in SQL). `getPunctualityBonusPage` reuses `lib/payroll/incidentSources.ts`'s shared SELECTs (also used by Faltas/Retardos).
- **"Recalcula" notice**: `isPunctualityBonusRecalculationNeeded` (status 2): today's result differs from the snapshot, or `punctuality_bonus_settings.updated_at` for the frequency is later than `MIN(calculated_at)`. Another frequency's config doesn't trigger it.
- **Screens**: Bonos tab (`periodo`, `resultado=conserva|pierde|no_evaluado`, `q`, `pagina`, 25/page); delays/absences columns link to Retardos/Faltas with the employee pre-searched; settings card/modal/log above the list, editable anytime. Procesar "Bono punt." column; Detalle `bono_puntualidad` line (shows "Perdido: N incidencias (máximo M)" with $0 when lost, intentionally breaking the zero-line-omitted convention).
- **Consequences**: no retroactive change once approved/paid. A dead/skewed checador or an undetected holiday can wrongly remove the bonus branch-wide — mark "No aplica" in Faltas. Vacation and active economic days are neutral (see their sections). A mid-period hire is never evaluated.
- **Out of scope**: own tolerance, proration, fiscal payroll, cash/transfer split, per-branch/employee/role config, justifying from Bonos, manual grant/removal, recalculating only the bonus, validity dates/history beyond the log, second-block delays/early departures.
- **Known risks**: chains onto the batch — verify by diffing snapshots. Absence/delay detection is now copied across the calculation and three Recalcula modules — keep in sync.

## Bono de asistencia

Same screen, second tab (`?bono=asistencia`); actions in `nomina/bonos/actions.ts`. Rules of origin: `references/docs/asistencia.md`, `references/nomina/reglas_modulo_nomina.md` §2.2. A podólogo present the whole period with **zero** unjustified absences earns a configurable bonus; a single absence removes it. **Does not depend on `lateness_settings`** — delays don't affect this bonus.

- **Who's evaluated**: same controlled population, only if the frequency has an active `attendance_bonus_settings` row, `fecha_ingreso <= fecha_inicio`, at least one schedule row. Otherwise "No evaluado" (`bonus_not_configured`, `joined_mid_period`, `no_schedule`). `'F'` always "No evaluado".
- **Absences** = unjustified absences in range (working day = has a schedule, up to yesterday). Justified days don't count; **days already discounted by another period do count**. Delays never count.
- **Result**: 0 absences keeps `monto`, ≥1 loses it. No maximum, no proration.
- **Configuration + log**: `attendance_bonus_settings` (PK `id_empresa`+`id_payment_period`): `monto`, `status`, audit; `CK_attendance_bonus_settings_valores` requires `monto > 0`. Seeds: weekly+biweekly, $500, active. Works exactly like `updatePunctualityBonusSettings` minus the maximum.
- **Snapshot**: `bono_asistencia_resultado`, `_faltas`, `importe_bono_asistencia`; `CK_period_employees_bono_asistencia` ties it together. No breakdown table/lock.
- **Calculation**: in SQL (`lib/payroll/attendanceBonusSql.ts`: `ATTENDANCE_BONUS_APPLY_SQL`/`COLUMNS`), counting `#absences` rows **without** the discount lock. **Calculating before the period ends pays with partial data** — same caveat as puntualidad.
- **Source of truth**: `lib/payroll/attendanceBonus.ts`.
- **"Recalcula" notice**: `isAttendanceBonusRecalculationNeeded` (status 2): today's result differs, or `attendance_bonus_settings.updated_at` for the frequency is later than `MIN(calculated_at)`. Another frequency's config, or the puntualidad config, doesn't trigger it.
- **Screens**: `bono=puntualidad|asistencia` selector (`readBonusKind` in `lib/payroll/bonusUrls.ts`, default puntualidad); `BonusKindTabs` keeps `periodo`/`resultado`/`q`. Asistencia tab (`periodo`, `resultado`, `q`, `pagina`, 25/page); absences link to Faltas. Generic settings components (`BonusSettingsCard/Modal/Log`) take `bonusKind`, "Máximo de incidencias" only shows for `punctuality`. Procesar "Bono asist." column; Detalle `bono_asistencia` line ("Perdido: N faltas" with $0, intentional non-omission).
- **Consequences/out of scope/known risks**: same shape as puntualidad (no maximum, no `'J'`-removes-bonus feature, no proration, same checador/holiday/vacation/economic-day caveats); detection now copied across four Recalcula modules — keep in sync.

## Exclusión de vacaciones

Days inside a captured vacation block (`RH.vacation_entitlements`) are never an absence or lateness — discount no salary, don't remove the punctuality/attendance bonuses. No DB change; the rule only reads the table.

- **Rule**: a day is "de vacaciones" when a block has `fecha_inicio IS NOT NULL` and the day is in `[fecha_inicio, fecha_fin]`, regardless of status (`programada`, `en_curso`, `tomada`, `por_pagar`/`pagada`). Days paid in cash (`dias_pagados`) are **not** vacation days. A cancelled block stops excluding; a rescheduled one excludes only its new dates.
- **Vacation wins over the punch**: a late `entrada` on a vacation day is not lateness either — fix by rescheduling/cancelling the block (allowed only before it starts).
- **One SQL definition**: `NOT_ON_VACATION_CONDITION` (`lib/payroll/vacationExclusionSql.ts`) combined with the economic-day rule (below) into `NOT_ON_PAID_LEAVE_CONDITION` (`lib/payroll/paidLeaveExclusionSql.ts`) — **the combined constant** is what six places import (never `NOT_ON_VACATION_CONDITION` directly): `#absences`/`#lateness` in `calculatePayrollPeriod`, and the four Recalcula notices (absence, lateness, both bonuses).
- **Screens**: `loadVacationBlocks`/`buildVacationDatesByEmployee` feed the optional `paidLeaveDates` parameter of the detection functions (empty set by default). **Faltas** detects *without* exclusion and splits out these dates as read-only "Vacaciones" rows (no actions, `estado=vacaciones` filter, its own card). Retardos/Bonos just exclude the days silently.
- **Writes**: justification actions call `validateDayIsNotOnVacation` after the target validation, returning `{ ok: false }` on a vacation day; the `clear*` actions skip this check.
- **Recalcula**: existing notices fire by themselves when a block touches an already-calculated period.
- **Consequences**: no retroactive change in approved/paid periods. Exclusion uses the date range, not counted days.
- **Out of scope**: shift-extension bonus (vacation days have no punch), overtime on vacation days with a punch, vacation premium/paid-vacation-days payment (own section), showing vacations elsewhere, a holiday catalog.
- **Known risks**: the `NOT EXISTS` runs per employee/day on a lightly-populated table; index if slow. Any `WHERE` change in `#absences`/`#lateness` must be diffed against an already-calculated snapshot.

## Exclusión de días económicos

An active economic day (`RH.economic_days`) is never an absence or lateness — "computar el día como trabajado" (`reglas_modulo_nomina.md` §2.7). No DB change; reads via `UX_economic_days_empleado_fecha_activo (id_empleado, fecha)`.

- **Rule**: a day is "día económico" when a row has `cancelado_at IS NULL` and matching `fecha`. Cancelling stops excluding (becomes an absence again on recalculation without a punch).
- **Paid, no new line**: salary = days − `dias_falta`, so the day is paid by not being an absence — no separate perception (would double-pay).
- **Economic day wins over the punch**, same as vacations.
- **One SQL definition**: `NOT_ON_ECONOMIC_DAY_CONDITION` and the combined `NOT_ON_PAID_LEAVE_CONDITION` live in `paidLeaveExclusionSql.ts` — the same six places import only the combined constant, so a future holiday catalog touches one file.
- **Screens**: `loadEconomicDays`/`buildEconomicDayDatesByEmployee`/`buildPaidLeaveDatesByEmployee` feed the same `paidLeaveDates` parameter. Faltas classifies vacaciones → día económico → justification in that order (a day that's both shows "Vacaciones"); economic days show as read-only "Día económico" rows with `estado=dia-economico` filter and own card.
- **Writes**: `validateDayIsNotOnEconomicDay` runs right after the vacation check with its own error message.
- **Out of scope/known risks**: same shape as vacations (blocking a past date with existing punches, holiday catalog, showing elsewhere are all out; the extra `NOT EXISTS` is cheap — ≤3 active rows/employee/year).

## Vacaciones y prima vacacional

Pays two concepts per **year of entitlement** (`RH.vacation_entitlements`), in both payrolls, each at its own frozen daily salary. Days actually taken are paid inside the base salary (not absences, per spec above) — not a separate line.

- **Concepts/events**: **Prima vacacional** (`'P'`): `ROUND(dias_derecho × salario_diario × 25/100, 2)` over **all** entitled days (LFT art. 80), paid **once** per year even if partially taken; event date = block's `fecha_inicio`, or `fecha_pago` with no block, or no premium with neither. **Días no disfrutados pagados** (`'D'`): `ROUND(dias_pagados × salario_diario, 2)`, event date = `fecha_pago` (from "Marcar como pagadas"). A partially-taken block can therefore pay the premium and the surplus-days line in different periods.
- **Which period pays**: whose `fecha_inicio..fecha_fin` contains the event date, for an employee with salary `>0` of the type, concept not already paid elsewhere for that type. A retroactive block (period never calculated) shows "Pendiente" in the employee's Vacaciones tab.
- **One SQL definition**: `VACATION_PAY_EVENTS_SQL` (`lib/payroll/vacationPaySql.ts`) returns `(id_empleado, id_vacation_entitlement, concepto, fecha_evento, dias)`, shared by the calculation and the Recalcula check. `vacationPay.ts` mirrors it in integer cents.
- **Tables**: `period_employees` gains `dias_prima_vacacional`, `importe_prima_vacacional`, `dias_vacaciones_pagadas`, `importe_vacaciones_pagadas`. `period_employee_vacations` is breakdown+lock: `UNIQUE (id_vacation_entitlement, concepto, tipo_nomina)`, `ON DELETE CASCADE` to the row, FK to the entitlement (never deleted — cancel/revert just nulls columns).
- **Lock**: `#vacation_pay` drops concepts already paid by **another** period for the same type, like overtime.
- **Fiscal** (spec 73 engine, no new logic): `UNION ALL`s for `id 7` "Prima vacacional" (clave 021, `'U'`, 15 UMA/year, `'A'`) and `id 14` "Vacaciones pagadas" (clave 001, `'N'`, 100% taxed), each only if `>0`. Constants in `lib/payroll/constants.ts`.
- **"Recalcula"** (`vacationPayRecalculation.ts`, status 2): an unpaid event, or a stored row no longer matching a current event's `fecha_evento`/`dias`.
- **Screens**: Procesar "Vacaciones" column (premium + paid days, part at 0 omitted); employee's Vacaciones tab shows per-year "Prima: Pagada en {código}/Pendiente/—" and "Días: Pagados en {código}/Marcados, sin nómina calculada"; "Marcar como pagadas" modal previews which period will pay it (never blocks saving).
- **Known risks/out of scope**: the taxed part adds whole to the weekly ISR base (no art. 174 proration) — can jump a bracket. Moving a block between calculated periods needs both recalculated. No retroactive change in approved/paid periods. Not included: adjustable base salary, premium of retroactive/"paid outside payroll" blocks, IMSS SBC integration, termination-proportional vacations/aguinaldo, prima dominical, CFDI vacation node, a separate line for days taken. Paying unused days in cash while employed conflicts with LFT art. 79 (a business-policy decision, not a bug).

## Bono por extensión de jornada

Same screen, third tab (`?bono=extension`); actions in `nomina/bonos/actions.ts`. Rules of origin: `references/docs/bono-extension.md`. A podólogo an administrator **deliberately assigned** earns `(salario_diario/8) × 2` for every scheduled day they punched in. An agreement, **not overtime** — never touches `overtime_authorizations` or the double-hours limit, and doesn't appear in Horas extra.

- **Who earns it**: operativa row only, for a controlled employee (same population as Faltas/Retardos) with `payroll.shift_extension_assignments.activo = 1` **at calculation time**. Covers the whole period, no validity dates. `'F'` rows never assigned.
- **Assignment + log**: `shift_extension_assignments` (PK `id_empleado`): `activo`, audit; no seeds, no row or `activo=0` = no bonus. `shift_extension_assignments_log` logs each change. `getShiftExtensionAssignmentsLog` filters by active branch (no `id_empresa`/`id_sucursal` column, joins `RH.empleados`).
- **Worked days**: date in `max(fecha_inicio, fecha_ingreso)`..`fecha_fin`, has a schedule row for its weekday **and** at least one punch of any type (incomplete counts). A rest day with a punch doesn't count; a scheduled day without a punch doesn't count even if justified/no-aplica. Current schedule used; mid-period hires prorated naturally.
- **Amount**: `ROUND(salario_diario × 2 × 1 × días / 8, 2)` with the frozen operativa salary (`SHIFT_EXTENSION_HOURS_PER_DAY=1`, `SHIFT_EXTENSION_PAY_MULTIPLIER=2`, not configurable). Days multiplied before dividing by 8 so only the total rounds.
- **Snapshot**: `bono_extension_asignado` (bit), `bono_extension_dias`, `importe_bono_extension`; `CK_period_employees_bono_extension` ties unassigned→0/0 and assigned→`'O'` only. The flag is stored apart from the days so "assigned, 0 days" differs from "not assigned" for the Recalcula notice. **No breakdown table, no pay-once lock** — days visible in the Asistencia tab.
- **Calculation**: `#shift_extension_days` built, three columns filled in the batch (`lib/payroll/shiftExtensionBonusSql.ts`: `SHIFT_EXTENSION_WORKED_DAYS_SQL`, `_BONUS_APPLY_SQL`, `_BONUS_COLUMNS`). **Calculating before period end pays with partial data.**
- **Capture the schedule with the extended hour included**: to avoid double-paying, the podólogo's schedule is captured already including the extra hour — otherwise Horas extra would detect that hour daily and an admin could authorize it on top of the bonus (detection is unchanged, doesn't subtract anything; review Horas extra before authorizing).
- **Source of truth**: `lib/payroll/shiftExtensionBonus.ts`. `getShiftExtensionBonusPage` reads the **current** `salario_diario` for the on-screen estimate (can differ from the stored amount after a salary change).
- **`setShiftExtensionAssignment`**: `assertPayrollAccess()`, `zod`, transaction checks employee active/branch/controlled, reads row `UPDLOCK, HOLDLOCK`, upserts only if changed + logs. Can change anytime regardless of period status.
- **"Recalcula" notice**: `isShiftExtensionBonusRecalculationNeeded` (status 2): today's assigned flag/days/amount differs from the snapshot. Uses the snapshot's `salario_diario`, so a salary change alone doesn't trigger it.
- **Screens**: `BONUS_KIND_URL_VALUES.shift_extension = "extension"`; `periodo`/`q` kept across tabs, `resultado` only between puntualidad/asistencia, `asignacion` only on extension. Extension tab (`periodo`, `asignacion=asignado|sin_asignar`, `q`, `pagina`, 25/page): table with schedule days, worked days, assigned flag, estimate, "Asignar"/"Quitar" button with in-UI confirmation; assignment log as `<details>`. Procesar "Bono ext." column; Detalle `bono_extension_jornada` line.
- **Consequences**: no retroactive change once approved/paid. A dead checador lowers every assigned podólogo's bonus. A podólogo losing their role-2 user stops earning it silently (resumes if the role returns). **Two overlapping periods of different frequency pay the same days twice** — no lock against it.
- **Out of scope**: validity dates, configurable hour/multiplier/fixed amount, auto-subtracting the extended hour from Horas extra, a breakdown table/pay-once lock, assigning from the employee's file, other roles, fiscal payroll, bulk assignment.
- **Known risks**: chains onto the batch — verify by diffing snapshots (only `calculated_at/by` and the new columns should differ).

## Parámetros fiscales

Screen `/dashboard/nomina/parametros-fiscales`, roles 1/4 only. Stores/edits yearly tax data; **does not calculate the ISR** (own section). Rules of origin: `reglas_modulo_nomina.md` §2.4, 3.3. Amounts are typed from DOF/INEGI/SAT — **nothing is preloaded** (exception: the seven IMSS rates/limits, which don't expire yearly, preloaded with `vigente_desde = 2026-01-01`).

- **Parameters**: `payroll.tax_parameters`, one row per `clave`+`vigente_desde` (`UQ_tax_parameters_clave_vigencia`), `valor > 0`, sixteen fixed keys (`CK_tax_parameters_clave`; `TAX_PARAMETER_KEYS` holds label/unit): `UMA_DIARIA/MENSUAL/ANUAL` (change 1-Feb), `SALARIO_MINIMO_GENERAL/FRONTERA` (1-Jan), `SUBSIDIO_MONTO_MENSUAL`, `SUBSIDIO_TOPE_INGRESO_MENSUAL`, `SUBSIDIO_FACTOR_DIAS_MES` (30.4), `DIAS_ANIO`, plus the 7 IMSS keys (see "IMSS" below). No company/branch columns — tax data is national.
- **Validity rule**: applicable value = row with latest `vigente_desde <= fecha_fin` (not `fecha_pago`/`fecha_corte`). No such row → resolver returns `null` and the calculation must refuse — **no defaults**. `resolveTaxParameter`/`resolveTaxParameterRow` (`lib/payroll/taxParameters.ts`, pure) — the latter returns the whole row so the ISR screen can show which `vigente_desde` was used.
- **`saveTaxParameter`/`deleteTaxParameter`**: `assertPayrollAccess()` + zod; transaction reads row `UPDLOCK, HOLDLOCK`, writes, logs (`valor_anterior NULL` = insert, `valor_nuevo NULL` = delete). Saving the same value writes nothing. Key is `(clave, vigente_desde)` — creating an existing one edits it; to move a value to another date, delete and recreate.
- **ISR tariff**: `payroll.tablas_retencion`, **weekly** (`clave_sat='02'`) **and monthly** (`'05'`, for the Christmas-bonus art. 174 ISR) — screen has a Semanal|Mensual selector, every write/gap-check/copy is per frequency. Resolved in SQL by `ejercicio`, `UQ_tablas_retencion_tramo (ejercicio, id_payment_period, limite_inferior)`. `decimal(18,2)`, bounds inclusive, SAT brackets contiguous by one cent.
  - **Writes** (`saveWithholdingBracket`, `deleteWithholdingBracket`, `copyWithholdingTableFromPreviousYear`): zod + SQL re-validation inside the transaction: `limite_inferior >= 0`, `limite_superior > limite_inferior` or NULL, `cuota_fija >= 0`, `porcentaje_excedente` 0–100, **no overlaps**, **at most one open bracket**. `id_tarifa` is `MAX+1` under `UPDLOCK, HOLDLOCK` (not an IDENTITY).
  - **Gaps are shown, not blocked** (blocking both overlaps and gaps would make a middle bracket uneditable). `findWithholdingGaps` flags them; `findWithholdingBracket` returns `null` inside a gap, so the calculation refuses. To change a limit: edit the next bracket's lower limit first (creating a flagged gap), then the previous bracket's upper limit.
  - **Copy** duplicates the previous year's brackets of the selected frequency with new ids (fails if target year has brackets or source has none); does not copy parameters.
  - `findWithholdingBracket(brackets, income)` picks the greatest `limite_inferior <= income` that contains it.
- **Exemption limits**: `payroll.perceptions` has a PK (`id_perception`) and four structured columns tied by `CK_perceptions_exencion`: `tipo_limite_exencion` (`'N'` none, `'T'` fully exempt, `'U'` UMA cap, `'P'` % of amount, `'M'` % with UMA cap), `umas_limite`, `porcentaje_exento`, `periodicidad_limite` (`'D'/'S'/'M'/'A'/'E'`). The `CHECK` spells out `IS NOT NULL` explicitly (else `NULL > 0` passes as UNKNOWN). `savePerception` mirrors the `CHECK` with a zod discriminated union; `PerceptionModal` shows only applicable fields. Captured: double overtime `'M'` (id 5, 50%, 5 UMA/week), triple overtime `'N'` (id 13, fully taxed), Sunday premium `'U'` (1 UMA/event), Christmas bonus `'U'` (30 UMA/year), PTU and vacation premium `'U'` (15 UMA/year); salary/commissions `'N'`. Ids live in `OVERTIME_DOUBLE/TRIPLE_PERCEPTION_ID`.
- **Screen**: `parametros-fiscales/page.tsx` (Server Component), state in URL (`pestana=parametros|tarifa|percepciones`, `ejercicio`), fetches only the active tab + log. Client: leaf modals/buttons with in-UI confirmations.
- **Out of scope**: tariffs for other frequencies, employer's IMSS, pre-2025 subsidy table, severance, automatic DOF/INEGI/SAT loading, per-branch parameters, retroactive changes in calculated periods.
- **Known risks**: a wrongly typed value breaks the whole fiscal payroll's withholdings — the log records who/what, verify against the SAT simulator. **Forgetting February's new UMA** silently uses January's. Editing a parameter never changes an already-calculated period until it's recalculated.

## ISR

The ISR to withhold on every fiscal row: article-96 tariff on salary minus employment subsidy, **no balance in the employee's favor**. Rules of origin: employment-subsidy decree (DOF 31-dec-2025), RMF 2026 Annex 8, `reglas_modulo_nomina.md` §3.3. Operativa rows never have ISR.

- **Rule**: **taxable base** = `ISNULL(SUM(importe_gravado), 0)` over `period_employee_perceptions` (see "Gravado/exento" below) — today just the salary, fully taxed. **Tariff**: `tablas_retencion` of the period's frequency with `ejercicio = YEAR(fecha_fin)` (not `periods.ejercicio`, so a New-Year-crossing week uses one consistent year); bracket via `findWithholdingBracket`; applied as-is, **not prorated by days**. **ISR causado** = `ROUND(cuota_fija + (base - limite_inferior) × porcentaje/100, 2)`. **Subsidy**: the three parameters resolved at `fecha_fin`; right to it when `base × factor <= tope × dias` (compared without divisions, so SQL/TS never differ at the border); `subsidio_causado = ROUND(monto × dias/factor, 2)` where `dias` = paid days (`pe.dias`, lateness doesn't subtract days). **Withheld** = `causado - min(causado, causado)` i.e. causado minus the smaller of subsidy/causado — only ever brings ISR to 0, never paid out. **Base 0** stays `'C'` with bracket/ISR/subsidy/withheld all 0 (the tariff starts at 0.01, not treated as a gap).
- **"ISR no calculado"**, row stays `'N'` with a reason, no deduction, in order: `no_withholding_table` (no tariff for the frequency/year — today every biweekly period), `missing_subsidy_parameters`, `income_in_gap`, and (checked before the gap) `exemption_not_calculated` when any perception row is `'N'` (base unknown; `CK_period_employees_isr` requires `NULL` base with that reason). **No defaults.** Approving a period with `'N'` rows isn't blocked (spec 94 chose to warn only).
- **Snapshot**: `period_employees` gains `isr_estado` (`'X'` default/operativa, `'C'`, `'N'`), `isr_motivo`, `isr_base_gravable`, frozen bracket (`isr_ejercicio_tarifa`, `isr_limite_inferior`, `isr_cuota_fija`, `isr_porcentaje_excedente`), `isr_causado`, three frozen subsidy parameters each with `*_vigente_desde`, `subsidio_con_derecho`, `subsidio_causado`, `subsidio_aplicado`, `isr_retenido`. `CK_period_employees_isr` ties it all together, explicit `IS NOT NULL`.
- **Deductions**: `period_employee_deductions` (`id_period_employee`, `id_deduction` FK to `deductions`, `importe >= 0`, `UQ (id_period_employee, id_deduction)`, `ON DELETE CASCADE`): `id_deduction = 2` ISR and (separately) `= 1` IMSS, written **only for `'C'` rows, even at 0** (so "calculated and 0" differs from "not calculated"). No pay-once lock — belongs to its own row. **Net** = `total_percepciones - ISNULL(SUM(importe), 0)`.
- **Calculation**: in the batch, after the snapshot `INSERT` and the perception rows: `UPDATE` of `'F'` rows filling the breakdown, then `INSERT` into deductions for `'C'` ones. Snippets in `lib/payroll/isrSql.ts` (server-only), shared with the Recalcula notice.
- **Source of truth**: `lib/payroll/isrCalculation.ts` (`calculateIsrBreakdown`, scaled integers, pure).
- **"Recalcula" notice**: `isIsrRecalculationNeeded` (status 2 only): recomputes every `'F'` row's breakdown and compares 18 columns with `EXCEPT` (treats two NULLs as equal). `tablas_retencion` has no `updated_at`, so changes are only caught this way. An `'F'` row in `'X'` also triggers it.
- **Screens** (fiscal only): Procesar "ISR"/"Neto" columns ("No calculado"/"—"); summary cards add "ISR retenido"/"Neto a pagar"; `IsrNotCalculatedNotice` with per-reason counts and links. Detalle: `PayrollDeductionsCard`.
- **Out of scope**: other frequencies/the annual tariff, IMSS (own section, doesn't change the ISR base), monthly/annual adjustment and day proration, ISR on operativa, receipts/CFDI/"otros pagos", manual adjustments, retroactive changes in approved/paid periods.
- **Known risks**: a wrongly typed parameter/bracket breaks the whole fiscal payroll — confirm against the SAT simulator before the first real payroll. Tariff changes leave no trail in the table itself (the snapshot freezes the bracket; the notice catches differences). **Forgetting January's subsidy or the year's tariff** leaves rows "No calculado".

## Gravado/exento

Each perception of a fiscal row is stored as its own row in `payroll.period_employee_perceptions` with taxed/exempt amounts, computed by a **generic engine** reading only the structured exemption rule of the `perceptions` catalog. CFDI nómina 1.2 requires both amounts per perception. Rules of origin: LISR art. 93, `reglas_modulo_nomina.md` §2.4. Operativa rows never have perception rows.

- **Today generated**: salary (`id 1`); Christmas bonus (`id 2`, only in an aguinaldo period) and PTU (`id 3`, only in a PTU period); double overtime (`id 5`, `'M'` 50% with 5-UMA weekly cap) and triple overtime (`id 13`, `'N'` 100% taxed), each only when `>0`, `eventos = NULL`. ISR base = salary + taxed overtime. New fiscal perceptions just add a `UNION ALL` to `#fiscal_perception_lines (id_period_employee, id_perception, importe, eventos)` and an entry in `PERCEPTION_LINE_KEY_BY_ID`, which ties each stored row to its Detalle line.
- **Engine** (no per-concept logic):

  | `tipo_limite_exencion` | Exempt |
  |---|---|
  | `N` none | 0 |
  | `T` total | `importe` |
  | `P` percentage | `ROUND(importe × % / 100, 2)` |
  | `U` UMA cap | `min(importe, remaining cap)` |
  | `M` % with cap | `min(ROUND(importe × % / 100, 2), remaining cap)` |

  **Taxed = importe − exempt** (never rounded separately, so they always sum to `importe`). **Window cap** (`U`/`M`) = `ROUND(umas_limite × UMA_DIARIA × multiplier, 2)`; multiplier is `eventos` in `'E'`, paid days (`pe.dias`) in `'D'`, 1 in `'S'/'M'/'A'`. **Remaining cap** = `max(0, cap − previously consumed)`. **Previously consumed**: 0 in `'E'/'D'/'S'`; in `'A'`/`'M'` it's the sum of `importe_exento` of the same perception+employee in `'C'` rows of other, **earlier** periods of the same year (and month for `'M'`), tie-broken by `id_period` so order of calculation doesn't matter — not filtered by branch/frequency (the cap is per person). **UMA**: `UMA_DIARIA` at `fecha_fin`, frozen with its `vigente_desde`.
- **"Exento no calculado"**: `'N'` with a reason and no amounts when type is `U`/`M` with no `UMA_DIARIA` (`missing_uma`, checked first), or periodicity `'S'` with a non-weekly period frequency (`unsupported_frequency`). `N`/`T`/`P` always calculate. No defaults. A `'N'` row makes the employee's ISR `'N'`/`exemption_not_calculated`.
- **Table**: one row per perception+`'F'` row (`UQ (id_period_employee, id_perception)`, `importe > 0`, `ON DELETE CASCADE`). Freezes the catalog rule used, the UMA with `vigente_desde`, the cap and previously-consumed amount. `CK_period_employee_perceptions_regla`/`_exencion` tie it together with explicit `IS NOT NULL`.
- **Calculation**: in the batch, after the snapshot `INSERT` and before the ISR `UPDATE`. Snippets in `lib/payroll/exemptionSql.ts` (server-only), shared with the notice.
- **Source of truth**: `lib/payroll/exemptionCalculation.ts`, pure, scaled integers.
- **"Recalcula" notice**: `isExemptionRecalculationNeeded` (status 2): recomputes each row with today's catalog/UMA/accumulation and compares via `EXCEPT`. An `'F'` row with salary but no salary-perception row (pre-this-spec snapshot) also triggers it.
- **Screens** (fiscal only): Detalle shows "Gravado"/"Exento" per line with a plain-language rule note, and taxed/exempt totals (the taxed total equals the ISR deductions-card base). Parámetros fiscales Percepciones tab notes a change only applies to recalculated periods.
- **Out of scope**: the general social-welfare/food-voucher limit (`id_perception=9` treated fully taxed despite a legacy flag), IMSS integration (own engine, doesn't reuse `importe_exento`) or SBC integration, tariffs of other frequencies, approval/payment, receipts/CFDI, a catalog log, taxed/exempt on operativa.
- **Known risks**: a wrongly typed catalog rule over- or under-exempts the whole fiscal payroll (no catalog log — the frozen row and the notice are the only safety net). Year/month accumulation uses `fecha_fin`, not `fecha_pago` — **if ever changed, change both** `ISR_PARAMETERS_SQL` and `EXEMPTION_APPLY_SQL` at once (confirmed 2026-10-13). The subsidy right compares against the **taxed** base — a growing taxed base can lose the subsidy intentionally. **Forgetting February's UMA** keeps caps low. Two simultaneous calculations for the same employee can read each other's stale accumulation; the later period's notice catches it.

## Aguinaldo

The yearly Christmas bonus is paid in an **extraordinary payroll**: `tipo_periodo = 'E'`, `concepto_extraordinario = 'A'` (the PTU shares the type with concept `'P'`, below — anything filtering `tipo_periodo = 'E'` for the bonus now also filters concept `'A'`). Reuses the snapshot, statuses, Procesar, Detalle and the gravado/exento engine. Rules of origin: `references/docs/aguinaldo.md` (LFT art. 87, LISR art. 93, RLISR art. 174). The termination-proportional aguinaldo is a separate spec (finiquito).

- **Period type**: `tipo_periodo` (`'O'` default, `'E'`; `PayrollPeriodType`). An `'E'` period uses SAT periodicity **99** (`EXTRAORDINARY_PERIOD_SAT_KEY`, inserted into `RH.payment_periods` with `status=0` so it's never offered as an employee frequency), covers 1-jan to 31-dec of its `ejercicio` (`CK_periods_extraordinaria_fechas`), code `NOM-{year}-E{NN}`. `UX_periods_extraordinaria_sucursal_ejercicio_concepto` (filtered unique index) allows **one per branch, year and concept**, so aguinaldo and PTU periods coexist; `createPayrollPeriod` re-checks it in-transaction.
- **Create/edit/delete**: Periodos modal has Ordinaria|Aguinaldo|PTU. In E: year instead of frequency, start/end fixed, cutoff/payment suggested 20-dec and editable (a later payment just warns). Edit changes only cutoff/payment; the usual status-1-only rule applies. List shows an "Aguinaldo" (violet) or "PTU" (teal) badge; ordinary-only screens ignore E periods.
- **Calculation** forks by `tipo_periodo`/`concepto` into `calculateExtraordinaryPayrollPeriod` ('A') or `calculatePtuPaymentPeriod` ('P') (`CHRISTMAS_BONUS_SNAPSHOT_SQL`). Standard transaction pattern; deletes snapshot, inserts bonus rows.
  - **Who**: active employees of the branch, `fecha_ingreso <= fecha_fin`, salary `>0` of the type, **any payment frequency, even none** (`ELIGIBLE_CHRISTMAS_BONUS_EMPLOYEE_CONDITIONS`). Terminated employees excluded (their bonus is in the finiquito spec). Branch changes mid-year: collected entirely in the current branch.
  - **Days** = `fecha_fin - max(fecha_inicio, fecha_ingreso) + 1` (counted to 31-dec regardless of calculation date; **absences not deducted**). **Amount** = `ROUND(salario × 15 × días/365, 2)`, 365 fixed even in a leap year. No variable salary.
  - **Rows**: `period_employees` gains `dias_aguinaldo`, `importe_aguinaldo` (0 in ordinary periods). In an E row every ordinary concept is 0 and `isr_estado='X'`. `CK_period_employees_montos` was relaxed to allow `dias=0` with `importe_salario=0` when `dias_aguinaldo > 0`.
  - **Taxed/exempt**: one perception line `id 2` (SAT 002, `'U'`, 30 UMA/year, `'A'`); exempt = `min(importe, 30×UMA − exempt already used that year)`. Only the first payment of the year uses the full 30 UMA.
- **ISR art. 174 RLISR** (`isrArticle174Sql.ts`/`isrArticle174.ts`, pure mirror; SQL wins on divergence). On the taxed amount, with the **monthly** tariff of the year and **no subsidy**: (1) ordinary monthly income = `ROUND(salario_diario_fiscal × 30.4, 2)` (fixed 30.4, `ARTICLE_174_MONTH_DAYS_FACTOR`, not the parameter); (2) monthly portion = `ROUND(taxed × 30.4/365, 2)`; (3) ISR of income alone and income+portion via the normal bracket formula; (4) rate = `ROUND(diff/portion, 6)`, withheld = `ROUND(taxed × rate, 2)`. Taxed 0 → calculated at 0, no bracket.
  - **Storage**: `period_employee_isr_article174` (one row/`'F'` row of an E period, `ON DELETE CASCADE`), freezing every step and both brackets. The spec-70 `isr_*` columns stay `'X'`. Withheld amount also written as deduction `id 2`, only for `'C'` rows (even at 0). No ISR on operativa.
  - **"No calculado"**, in order: `exemption_not_calculated`, `no_monthly_withholding_table`, `income_in_gap`. No defaults.
- **Monthly tariff**: Parámetros fiscales ISR tab has Semanal|Mensual (`?frecuencia=semanal|mensual`); writes/overlaps/gaps/copy work per frequency. Weekly ISR (own section) already filters by `id_payment_period`, so loading the monthly tariff doesn't affect it.
- **Screens**: Procesar with an E period: `ChristmasBonusEmployeesTable` (Días, Salario diario, Aguinaldo; fiscal adds Gravado/Exento/ISR/Neto) and dedicated summary cards. `IsrNotCalculatedNotice` uses the art. 174 reasons. The ordinary Recalcula notices are **not evaluated** for an E period (except exemption). Detalle: only the `aguinaldo` line, and `PayrollArticle174IsrCard` replaces `PayrollDeductionsCard` in fiscal; ordinary lists hidden. Horas extra/Faltas/Retardos/Bonos **don't list E periods**.
- **Notices**: **20-dec deadline** (`ChristmasBonusDeadlineNotice`, in Periodos+Procesar): from 1-dec, while the branch has **not created** the year's aguinaldo period, amber until 20-dec then red "venció". Creating the period removes it (not calculating). **"Recalcula"** (`isExtraordinaryRecalculationNeeded`, covers both concepts): for E periods at status 2, the daily salary/`fecha_ingreso` differ, or today's art. 174 breakdown differs from stored.
- **Out of scope**: finiquito/liquidation (separate spec), variable salary, several instalments, deducting absences, employment subsidy, IMSS, ISN, CFDI stamping, receipts/dispersion, Aprobada/Pagada statuses, annual ISR adjustment (art. 97), tariffs of other frequencies, splitting between branches.
- **Known risks**: a wrong/missing monthly tariff breaks the whole bonus's withholding (confirm with the accountant/SAT simulator first). An employee terminated after calculation keeps the full-year bonus — the finiquito spec must subtract it, and recalculating the E period drops them. A re-hire's finiquito paying a proportional bonus in the same year would double-grant the 30 UMA cap across the two employee records (open decision). `calculatePayrollPeriod` gained a whole branch — diff ordinary-period snapshots before/after any change here.

## PTU: reparto

PTU is **distributed per company (RFC) and fiscal year**, not per branch, in `payroll.ptu_distributions`. Rules of origin: LFT arts. 117–131 (2021 reform). **This spec only computes who gets how much and closes the result; paying it is a separate spec** ("PTU: pago", below). Fiscal payroll only.

- **Tables**: `ptu_distributions` (one per `id_empresa`+`ejercicio`, `UQ_ptu_distributions_empresa_ejercicio`; state `'B'` draft/`'C'` closed + derived totals), `ptu_distribution_employees` (one row/worker: inputs, frozen employee data, derived columns), `ptu_history` (PTU of earlier years typed by hand, PK `id_empleado`+`ejercicio`), `ptu_distribution_log` (`ON DELETE CASCADE`). `CHECK`s enforce: `total_repartido + remanente_topes + remanente_redondeo = fondo`, `mitad_dias + mitad_salario = fondo`, `factor_tres_meses IN (90, 91.25)`, an ineligible row has a reason and `ptu_final = 0`.
- **Employee category**: `RH.empleados.categoria_ptu` (`'N'` normal default, `'C'` trust, `'X'` excluded — director/admin/GM), edited in the employee's "Datos fiscales" (see `docs/rh-empleados.md`). Frozen into each row on every recalculation.
- **Parameters**: `ejercicio` (fixed once created), `tipo_persona` (`'M'`/`'F'`, used for the payment deadline), `renta_gravable >= 0` (typed already computed — not derived from the tax return), `ptu_no_cobrada_anterior >= 0` (art. 122), `factor_tres_meses` (90 or 91.25).
- **Fund/halves**: `fondo = ROUND(renta_gravable × 10%, 2) + ptu_no_cobrada_anterior`; `mitad_dias = ROUND(fondo/2, 2)`; `mitad_salario = fondo - mitad_dias`.
- **Preload** (`preloadPtuDistribution`): employees of the company's branches with ≥1 `'F'` row of an **ordinary** period whose `fecha_fin` falls in the year (active or terminated). `dias = SUM(dias)` (capped at 366), `salario = SUM(importe_salario)` (salary only — no overtime/bonuses/commissions/aguinaldo/vacation), `salario_diario_tope` = latest `'F'` row's `salario_diario`. **Adds missing workers, updates only non-`editado` rows, never deletes.** Idempotent — unchanged payroll → no changes, no log entry. Company = employee's **current** branch.
- **Add by hand** (`addPtuDistributionEmployee`): active employees hired by 31-dec, no active termination before 1-jan, not already listed — enter at 0 days/salary, `salario_diario_tope = salario_diario_fiscal`, `origen = 'M'`. Only hand-added rows can be removed (drop a preloaded one by zeroing its days).
- **Edit a row** (`updatePtuDistributionEmployee`): days/salary/cap; sets `editado = 1`, one log entry per changed field.
- **Eligibility**, in order: `excluded_category` (`'X'`), `eventual_under_60_days` (non-`'01'` contract and <60 days; `NULL` contract counts as `'01'`), `no_days`. Ineligible → $0 + reason, no totals.
- **120% cap for trust workers**: `tope_salario_diario_confianza = ROUND(1.2 × highest eligible 'N' salario_diario_tope, 2)`; a `'C'` row distributes `min(salario, cap × dias)`. No eligible `'N'` rows → no cap.
- **Gross share**: `ptu_dias = TRUNC(mitad_dias × dias/total_dias)`, `ptu_salario = TRUNC(mitad_salario × salario_reparto/total_salario_reparto)` — **truncated, not rounded**, so the sum never exceeds the fund. Fund/halves/caps/average round half up.
- **Caps (art. 127 fr. VIII)**: `tope_tres_meses = ROUND(salario_diario_tope × factor, 2)`; `promedio_tres_anios` = average of years −1/−2/−3 PTU, divided only by years with data; cap = the greater, `ptu_final = min(bruto, cap)`.
- **Remainder, not redistributed**: `remanente_topes = SUM(bruto - final)`, `remanente_redondeo = fondo - SUM(bruto)` (soaking up any undistributed halves too, to satisfy the `CHECK`). The not-redistributing criterion is **unconfirmed with the accountant**.
- **History** (`getPtuHistory`/`savePtuHistory`): per year, a company's **closed** distribution's `ptu_final` wins over manual capture (which is then rejected); with no closed distribution, `ptu_history` applies; neither → "no data". A closed `ptu_final = 0` counts as a $0 data point. Same-value save writes nothing; `importe: null` deletes.
- **Recalculation on every write**: no "Calcular" button — each action locks the distribution (`UPDLOCK, HOLDLOCK`), applies its change, calls `recalculatePtuDistribution` and writes every derived column. **Calculation lives only in TypeScript** (`calculatePtuDistribution`, pure, integer cents, `BigInt` for salary share) — **no SQL mirror**, unlike other specs, because of the chained per-row caps. Don't `Promise.all` a transaction-bound client.
- **Life cycle**: draft = fully editable. `closePtuDistribution` requires `renta_gravable > 0` and ≥1 eligible worker; recalculates first (freezing branch/category/contract as of today), writes `cerrado_by/at`. Closed = read-only for every action, even a stale tab. `reopenPtuDistribution` is rejected while a calculated PTU period (status >= 2) uses the distribution ("El reparto ya se usa en la nómina {código}… Revierte ese cálculo para reabrirlo"). `deletePtuDistribution` only in draft, cascades.
- **Log**: `P` parameters, `R` preload, `A` add, `Q` remove, `E` row edit, `H` history, `C` close, `O` reopen — same-value saves write nothing.
- **Screens/code**: `nomina/ptu/page.tsx` (list) and `nomina/ptu/[id]/page.tsx` (detail: parameters, cards, 13-column workers table, log, Close/Reopen/Delete), both Server Components; client pieces are the modals/action buttons. Actions in `nomina/ptu/actions.ts`, schemas in `lib/payroll/ptuSchemas.ts`, types in `interfaces/payroll_ptu.ts`, constants `PTU_*`. Roles 1/4, `assertPayrollAccess()`, company from session; a distribution of another company is a 404.
- **Out of scope**: paying PTU (next section), operativa PTU, computing taxable income or applying losses/new-company exemption, redistributing the remainder, auto-carrying unclaimed PTU, recording disabilities (edited by hand instead), variable-salary averaging, mixed commission, the draft-for-objections stage, receipts, splitting a worker between branches.
- **Known risks**: remainder criterion unconfirmed (stored, so changing it is just a recalculation). A wrong `renta_gravable` misallocates the whole company (logged, and it's the first card shown). A fiscal payroll started mid-year preloads fewer days/salary than real — complete by hand before closing. A director left as `'N'` collects PTU; a trust worker left as `'N'` raises others' cap — review categories before preloading. Recalculating an ordinary period after preload doesn't reach non-edited rows until the next preload. Someone who moved companies disappears from the old company's preload.

## PTU: pago

A **closed** distribution is paid in an **extraordinary fiscal payroll per branch** in the payment year: `tipo_periodo = 'E'`, `concepto_extraordinario = 'P'`. Reuses the snapshot, statuses, Procesar, Detalle, the gravado/exento engine and the art. 174 ISR of Aguinaldo unchanged. **The period doesn't recalculate the distribution — it pays the closed `ptu_final`.** Rules of origin: LFT arts. 117–131 and 122 (payment within 60 days of the annual return), LISR art. 93, RLISR art. 174.

- **Period**: SAT 99, `ejercicio` = **payment year**, 1-jan to 31-dec fixed, code `NOM-{year}-E{NN}` sharing the consecutive with that year's aguinaldo. Pays the branch's company's distribution for `ejercicio - 1`. One PTU period per branch/year. Usual edit/delete rules.
- **Create** (`createExtraordinaryPayrollPeriod`, concept `'P'`): in-transaction (`PTU_PAYMENT_PRECONDITION_SQL`), the previous year's distribution must be **closed** and the branch must have ≥1 row with `ptu_final > 0`. Suggested payment=cutoff = the deadline for the distribution's `tipo_persona`; a later date only warns.
- **Deadline**: `getPtuPaymentDeadline(year, taxpayerType)` — 30-may (persona moral) / 29-jun (persona física) (LFT art. 122). `getPtuPaymentDeadlineState`: `none`/`upcoming` (30 days before)/`overdue` (day after).
- **Calculation** (`calculatePtuPaymentPeriod`, `PTU_PAYMENT_SNAPSHOT_SQL`): standard transaction, period and distribution both locked, distribution required `'C'`.
  - **Who**: distribution rows with **frozen** `id_sucursal` = the period's branch and `ptu_final > 0`, **terminated workers included**; current employee status/frequency/branch irrelevant. **`'F'` only.**
  - **Amount** = `ptu_final` as is; `importe_ptu` on the snapshot (0 elsewhere); `salario_diario` = the frozen `salario_diario_tope`; every ordinary concept is 0, `isr_estado='X'`.
  - **`period_employee_ptu`** (one row/`'F'` row, `ON DELETE CASCADE`) links to the distribution row and freezes the breakdown; `UQ_period_employee_ptu_distribution_employee` makes each distribution row payable **once in all history**.
  - **Zero cap**: a payable row with `salario_diario_tope = 0` writes nothing and the action lists the names, telling the user to reopen/fix/close the distribution first.
  - **Taxed/exempt**: one line `id 3` (SAT 003, `'U'`, 15 UMA/year, `'A'`); exempt = `min(importe, 15×UMA - exempt used that year)`.
  - **ISR art. 174**: same SQL as Aguinaldo, no subsidy — ordinary monthly income = `ROUND(salario_diario_tope × 30.4, 2)` (the row's frozen cap), monthly tariff of the payment year. Same breakdown table, deduction, "No calculado" handling.
  - "Revertir" works like any period; recalculating replaces the snapshot without hitting the `UNIQUE`.
- **Reopen lock**: `reopenPtuDistribution` rejected while a PTU period at status >= 2 is linked (`findPtuDistributionPayrollUsage`); reverting that period frees it.
- **Procesar**: a PTU period always shows fiscal only (`tipo` toggle hidden). `PtuPaymentEmployeesTable`: Días, PTU por días/salario, PTU (marks "Topado"), Gravado, Exento, ISR, Neto; dedicated summary cards. No "employees without salary" notice in a PTU period.
- **Detalle**: one `ptu` line "PTU {year-1}" with the days/salary split and, if capped, which cap applied; `PayrollArticle174IsrCard` with a `concept` prop for text only; toggle and ordinary lists hidden.
- **Notices**: **PTU deadline** (`PtuPaymentDeadlineNotice`): only when the company has a closed previous-year distribution, the branch has payable rows, and no PTU period yet created — amber from 30 days before, red after. **"Recalcula"** compares only today's art. 174 breakdown with stored (same function as Aguinaldo).
- **Other screens**: Horas extra/Faltas/Retardos/Bonos don't list E periods of either concept.
- **Access/validation**: roles 1/4, company from branch (never session/client input), zod on every input.
- **Out of scope**: recalculating/adjusting distribution amounts from the period, operativa PTU, several instalments, splitting a worker between branches, unclaimed PTU (typed by hand into the next distribution), paying terminated workers outside payroll/finiquito, employment subsidy, IMSS/ISN, CFDI/receipts/dispersion, Aprobada/Pagada statuses, blocking a late payment (only warns).
- **Known risks**: a wrongly closed distribution is paid as-is (can still be reopened/fixed until the period is calculated). The **payment** year's monthly tariff and UMA must both be loaded before paying (missing → "No calculado" / low exempt amounts). The art. 174 ordinary income uses the profits year's last fiscal salary, not the current one. Terminated workers without current contact info are still paid in their branch's payroll, but unclaimed PTU isn't tracked.

## IMSS

The worker's IMSS contribution (cuota obrera) withheld on every fiscal row of an **ordinary** period, any frequency, after the ISR. Its own deduction (`id 1`, SAT 001), **does not change the ISR base**. Operativa and extraordinary periods stay `'X'`. Rules of origin: `references/docs/imss.md`, **corrected against the LSS**: the source table lacked EyM cash benefits (0.25%, art. 107) and pensioners' medical expenses (0.375%, art. 25); CEAV worker contribution is a flat 1.125% (the progressive table is employer-only); the 2% Retiro is entirely employer-paid (art. 168 fr. I) and **not withheld**. Worked case (SBC $530, 15 days, UMA $117.31): withholding is **$199.50**, not the source document's $308.81.

- **Parameters**: seven keys in `tax_parameters` (`IMSS_OBRERO_EYM_EXCEDENTE` 0.40%, `_EYM_DINERO` 0.25%, `_EYM_PENSIONADOS` 0.375%, `_IV` 0.625%, `_CEAV` 1.125%, `IMSS_UMBRAL_EXCEDENTE_UMAS` 3, `IMSS_TOPE_SBC_UMAS` 25), stored as percentages, preloaded `vigente_desde = 2026-01-01` (no log row), editable with the same validity rule/log as the rest. `IMSS_TAX_PARAMETER_KEYS` lists them; missing one → `missing_imss_parameters`.
- **SBC**: typed by the accountant in the employee's **IMSS** tab (see `docs/rh-empleados.md`). The one in force at `fecha_fin` is used for the whole period (not split by days if it changes mid-period).
- **Rule** (`calculateImssBreakdown`, pure mirror):
  - **Who**: `tipo_regimen = '02'` only; others are `'X'`, no deduction. Regime/NSS read live, not frozen.
  - **Capped SBC** = `min(SBC, ROUND(IMSS_TOPE_SBC_UMAS × UMA, 2))`.
  - **Days**: EyM uses calendar days (`dias + dias_falta`); IV and CEAV use paid days (`dias`). Lateness subtracts no days.
  - **Branch amount** = `ROUND(daily base × days × rate/100, 2)`, each of the five branches rounded **separately** (EyM excess base `max(0, capped SBC - threshold×UMA)`; EyM cash benefits/pensioners/IV/CEAV base = capped SBC). **Accrued** = sum.
  - **Minimum wage (art. 36 LSS)**: `salario_diario <= SALARIO_MINIMO_GENERAL` at `fecha_fin` → **withheld = 0** (accrued still stored, flagged `exento_salario_minimo`, for employer-share visibility).
  - **Net cap**: withheld = `min(accrued, max(0, total perceptions - ISR withheld))` (ISR counts as 0 if not calculated). What doesn't fit is absorbed by the employer and **never collected later** (`topado_al_neto`).
- **"IMSS no calculado" (`'N'`)**, in order: `missing_nss` (regime 02, no NSS), `missing_sbc`, `missing_uma`, `missing_imss_parameters`, `missing_minimum_wage`. No defaults.
- **Snapshot**: `imss_estado` (`'X'`/`'C'`/`'N'`), `imss_motivo`, `imss_retenido` (`'C'` only), `CK_period_employees_imss`. `period_employee_imss` (`'C'` only, so every column is `NOT NULL`) freezes the whole breakdown (SBC+validity, capped SBC, UMA, threshold, cap, minimum wage+validity, days per group, rate/amount per branch, accrued, exemption/net-cap flags, withheld). Deduction row only for `'C'`, even at 0.
- **Calculation**: in the ordinary branch of the batch, **after** the ISR deduction: `UPDATE` of `'F'` rows, `INSERT` breakdown for `'C'` ones, `INSERT` their deduction. Snippets in `lib/payroll/imssSql.ts`, shared with the notice. Each branch multiplies in scale 6, divides by 100 after, so SQL doesn't truncate the intermediate division.
- **"Recalcula" notice**: `isImssRecalculationNeeded` (status 2 of an ordinary period): recomputes every `'F'` row and compares status/reason/30 columns via `EXCEPT`. Sees rate/UMA/minimum-wage/SBC/regime/NSS changes; an `'X'` row of a regime-02 employee (pre-this-spec) also triggers it.
- **Screens** (fiscal, ordinary only): Procesar "IMSS" column between ISR and Neto; card "IMSS retenido"; `ImssNotCalculatedNotice` with per-reason counts and links (employee's IMSS tab for `missing_nss`/`missing_sbc`, Parámetros fiscales for the rest). Detalle: IMSS line of `PayrollDeductionsCard`.
- **Out of scope**: employer contributions (EyM fixed fee, Retiro, progressive employer CEAV, guarderías, work risk, INFONAVIT 5%), computing the SBC itself, SUA/IDSE reconciliation (**differences never adjusted**), splitting the period when SBC changes mid-period, disabilities/the 7-day monthly absence limit, INFONAVIT/FONACOT loans, IMSS on operativa/extraordinary/finiquito, the border minimum wage, CFDI reporting of deduction 001.
- **Known risks**: the accountant must confirm the corrected rates before the first payroll (editable, so a later fix needs no code). **Employees without SBC** stay `'N'`/`missing_sbc` and withhold nothing until typed. A wrongly typed SBC is only warned about (non-blocking) — the Detalle shows the SBC used and the log records changes. **Forgetting February's UMA** leaves the threshold/cap stale. The net cap sums `period_employee_perceptions`, so a snapshot predating that spec (no perception row) understates the cap — already flagged by its own Recalcula notice.

## Timbrado CFDI (spec 98)

Stamps a CFDI 4.0 with the Nómina 1.2 complement, via Facturapi, for each fiscal row of an **ordinary** period in status 4 (Pagada) — one at a time or in batch. Extraordinary periods (aguinaldo, PTU) are out of scope. Uses the same organization/CSD/mode (Test or Live) as electronic invoicing — see `docs/facturacion.md`.

- **Table**: `payroll.period_employee_cfdi` — one row per vigente `(id_period_employee, livemode)` (`UX_period_employee_cfdi_vigente`, a filtered unique index over `estado IN ('P','T','E','X')`, so any number of cancelled rows can coexist with at most one vigente row per fold and mode; locked with `UPDLOCK, HOLDLOCK` on insert/update so two simultaneous clicks produce a single CFDI), `estado` `'P'`/`'T'`/`'E'`/`'X'`/`'C'` (processing/stamped/error/cancellation-pending/cancelled — spec 99 added the last two, `CHECK`), `facturapi_id`/`uuid`/`serie`/`folio`/`total`/`fecha_timbrado` only set from `'T'` onward (`CHECK`), `mensaje_error` only set on `'E'`. `id_period_employee` is nullable with `ON DELETE SET NULL` from `period_employees` (spec 99) — only a `'C'` row survives its row's deletion, carrying its own `id_period`/`id_empleado` so the Detalle can still find it. A filtered unique index enforces a UUID is never reused across rows.
- **Blockers** (`getPayrollStampingBlockReasons`, pure, `lib/payroll/cfdi/payrollStampingBlockers.ts`): period not ordinary, period not in status 4, incomplete/invalid employee fiscal data (reuses `getStampingIssues`, spec 71), ISR/IMSS not `'C'` (`'X'` IMSS is fine), an un-calculated gravado/exento on any perception, no active payroll issuer, or an issuer without a loaded CSD. `[]` means stampable; a blocked row never calls Facturapi.
- **Payload** (`buildPayrollCfdiPayload`, pure, `lib/payroll/cfdi/payrollCfdiPayload.ts`): type `N`, use `CN01`, series `NOM`, inline `customer`; one `Percepcion` per perception with amount `> 0`, `HorasExtra` sub-nodes per day/type (`01` dobles, `02` triples) with hours rounded **up** (`CEILING`) and the exact amount paid; one `Deduccion` per deduction `> 0`; `OtroPago` `002` (employment subsidy) only when `subsidio_con_derecho`, `Importe="0.00"`. Seniority as `P{weeks}W` (floored, never negative). Receiver name uppercased, single spaces, accents/Ñ kept. `Receptor/@RiesgoPuesto` comes from the **issuer's** `riesgo_puesto` (per the complement's own field mapping, not the employee's).
- **Repository** (`lib/payroll/cfdi/payrollCfdiRepository.ts`, `server-only`): `loadPayrollStampingSource` snapshots the fiscal data, the employee's current identity and the active issuer; `reservePayrollReceipt` inserts/updates the row to `'P'` inside a transaction (refuses an existing `'T'` or a `'P'` under `STALE_RESERVATION_MINUTES` (10); an older `'P'` needs `confirmStaleRetry`); `completePayrollReceipt` sets `'T'` and writes `payroll_receipt.stamp` to `audit_log` in the same transaction; `failPayrollReceipt` sets `'E'` with the PAC's message. `loadPeriodPayrollCfdiOverview` computes every row's `PayrollCfdiRowState` plus the active issuer's readiness in one pass — a single Facturapi call (the CSD check) for the whole period, reused by Procesar and by the employee Detalle.
- **Server actions** (`nomina/procesar/actions.ts`, roles 1/4): `stampPayrollReceipt({ idPeriod, idEmpleado, confirmStaleRetry? })` reserves the row, calls `getOrgClient(org_uid, idEmpresa).invoices.create(payload)`, and closes it `'T'`/`'E'`; `getPayrollStampingQueue({ idPeriod })` returns the `id_empleado` of every stampable pending/error row, in table order, for the batch button.
- **Reabrir lock**: `reopenPayrollPeriod` refuses (marker `PERIOD_HAS_STAMPED_RECEIPTS`) if the period has a **Live** receipt in `'T'` **or `'X'`** (spec 99 — while the SAT hasn't confirmed a cancellation, the CFDI is still vigente) — a stamped CFDI can't be un-happened. Test-only receipts don't block it; a recalculation deletes every non-`'C'` receipt of the period (see "Cancelación CFDI" below). Once every Live receipt is `'C'`, Reabrir works again.
- **Downloads**: `GET /api/nomina/cfdi/[id]/xml` and `.../pdf` (role 1/4, the only CRUD-adjacent REST endpoints besides `upload`/the ZKTeco webhook, since a download is a file response, not a Server Action): resolve tenant via the receipt → `RH.empleados.id_empresa`, 404 for another company's receipt, fetch from Facturapi with the receipt's own `org_uid` (not necessarily the org active today), and name the file `NOM-{folio}-{codigo_empleado}.xml|pdf`. They respond for `'T'`, `'X'` and `'C'` (spec 99 extended them past `'T'` so a cancelled receipt's original documents stay reachable); a third route, `.../acuse`, is spec 99's — see below.
- **Screens**: Procesar gains a "CFDI" column (fiscal view only) with a status badge (Pendiente/Bloqueado — tooltip with the reason(s) —/En proceso/Timbrado/Prueba/Cancelación en proceso/Cancelado/Error — tooltip with the PAC's message) and a Timbrar/Reintentar button on each stampable/failed row; a "Timbrar pendientes" button (status 4 only) stamps the queue in sequence showing "n/total" and the error count, with a "Detener" that finishes the in-flight receipt and stops; a notice when there's no active/certified issuer. Detalle gains `PayrollCfdiCard` below `PayrollDeductionsCard`, with the same states (blocked rows link to the expediente or Parámetros fiscales, like `IsrNotCalculatedNotice`) plus the XML/PDF links once `'T'`, Cancelar/Consultar estado buttons (spec 99), and a "Recibos cancelados" history below it.
- **Out of scope**: dispersion, batch concurrency (the queue is sequential on purpose), notifying anyone when a stamp fails. (CFDI cancellation and re-stamping after a cancellation are spec 99, below — no longer out of scope.)
- **Known risks**: Facturapi's nómina complement field names are typed by hand (the SDK types them as `any`); a sandbox PAC rejection would need a payload fix, not a schema change. The CSD check is a live Facturapi call on every page load of Procesar/Detalle (fiscal, ordinary) — acceptable for an admin tool, but not free.

## Cancelación CFDI (spec 99)

Cancels, one at a time, a nómina CFDI already stamped (`'T'`) in either mode, with SAT motive `02` or `03` — the two that don't require a substitute CFDI. The cancelled row and its cancellation receipt (acuse) are kept forever; the row's fiscal row goes back to "Pendiente" so it can be stamped again in the same period, without reopening it.

- **State machine**: `'T'` → `'X'` (cancellation requested, reserved with `UPDLOCK` before calling Facturapi so two clicks can't double-cancel) → `'C'` (SAT confirmed) or back to `'T'` (Facturapi/SAT rejected or the call failed). An `'X'` that got stuck because the process died mid-flight is reconciled the same way a stale `'P'` isn't: there's no 10-minute rule, just "Consultar estado".
- **Repository** (`lib/payroll/cfdi/payrollCfdiRepository.ts`): `reservePayrollCancellation` (`'T'` → `'X'`, refuses anything else), `completePayrollCancellation` (`'X'` → `'C'` plus `audit_log`, one transaction), `revertPayrollCancellation` (`'X'` → `'T'`, clears the four cancellation columns), `loadCancelledPayrollReceipts(idPeriod, idEmpleado, idEmpresa)` for the Detalle history. `reservePayrollReceipt`, `loadPeriodPayrollCfdiOverview` and `hasLiveStampedReceipts` all treat `'X'` as vigente (not stampable, counts for the Reabrir lock) and `'C'` as not vigente (the row looks "Pendiente" again). `DELETE_NON_CANCELLED_PERIOD_RECEIPTS_SQL` is interpolated into each of the four `DELETE FROM period_employees` batches in `procesar/actions.ts`, in the same transaction, so a recalculation drops every receipt except `'C'` ones (which detach via `ON DELETE SET NULL` instead of disappearing).
- **Pure rules** (`lib/payroll/cfdi/payrollCfdiCancellation.ts`, no SQL, importable from the client): `PAYROLL_CFDI_CANCELLATION_MOTIVE_OPTIONS` (filters `CANCELLATION_MOTIVE_OPTIONS` from `lib/billing/invoiceCatalogs.ts` down to `02`/`03`, no duplicated labels); `cancelPayrollReceiptSchema` (`idReceipt`, `motive` ∈ `02`/`03`, optional `confirmFolio`); `resolveCancellationOutcome(invoice)` → `'cancelled' | 'pending' | 'not_cancelled'` from Facturapi's `status`/`cancellation_status`; `formatPayrollReceiptFolio(receipt)` → `NOM-{folio}`, used for the Live confirmation.
- **Server actions** (`procesar/actions.ts`, roles 1/4): `cancelPayrollReceipt({ idReceipt, motive, confirmFolio? })` — in Live, requires `confirmFolio` to match before doing anything; reserves `'X'`; calls `getOrgClient(receipt.org_uid, idEmpresa).invoices.cancel(facturapi_id, { motive })` (always the receipt's saved issuer, never the one active today); resolves the outcome to `'C'`, leaves it `'X'`, or reverts to `'T'` with the PAC's message surfaced only in the dialog (never persisted); revalidates Procesar and the Detalle. `refreshPayrollCancellationStatus({ idReceipt })` — only for `'X'` rows — calls `invoices.retrieve` and resolves the same way.
- **UI**: `PayrollCfdiCancelDialog` (client, shared by Procesar's "CFDI" column and `PayrollCfdiCard`) shows employee, UUID, `NOM-{folio}`, issuer and mode; motive is a required radio pair; in Live, a text field must match the folio exactly before the confirm button enables; Facturapi's error, if any, renders inside the dialog. `PayrollCfdiCancelButton` opens it from a `'T'` row; `PayrollCfdiRefreshStatusButton` calls "Consultar estado" from an `'X'` row. `PayrollCfdiCard`'s "Recibos cancelados" subsection (Server Component) lists every `'C'` receipt of the period — UUID, serie-folio, mode, motive label, cancellation date and who cancelled — each with XML/PDF/Acuse links.
- **Downloads**: `GET /api/nomina/cfdi/[id]/acuse` — same role/`id_empresa` validation as `xml`/`pdf`, 404 unless the receipt is `'C'` — fetches `invoices.downloadCancellationReceiptXml` and names the file `NOM-{folio}-{codigo_empleado}-acuse.xml`.
- **Bitácora**: `payroll_receipt.cancel` in `BILLING.audit_log` (`target_id` = `uuid`, `mode` = test/live, `detail` = `{ id_period, id_empleado, facturapi_id, folio, motivo }`), written once, when the row reaches `'C'` — whether that happens from the cancel action directly or later from "Consultar estado".
- **Out of scope**: motive `01`/substitution (own spec — the substitute would come from a reopened period, and Reabrir is blocked while the original is still vigente), batch/"cancel all", automatic status polling (webhooks or scheduled jobs — only the manual button), persisting the last cancellation error, cancelling extraordinary-period receipts, notifying the employee.

## Detalle por empleado

Screen `/dashboard/nomina/procesar/[id_empleado]?periodo=ID&tipo=operativa|fiscal&puesto=&q=`; action `getPayrollEmployeeDetail(filters)` in `nomina/procesar/actions.ts`. Read-only: reads `payroll.period_employees`, `period_employee_treatments`, `period_employee_product_sales`, `payroll.periods`, `RH.empleados`, `RH.puestos` (plus `Tratamiento_onicomicosis`/`consultas`/`pacientes` for paid treatments, `inventory.Products` for sold-product names).

- **Parameters**: `periodo` required (no "current period" guessing). `tipo` defaults to `operativa`. `puesto`/`q` carried over for navigation only. URLs built with `buildPayrollProcessHref`/`buildPayrollEmployeeDetailHref` (`lib/payroll/processUrls.ts`).
- **404** (`notFound()`): missing/non-numeric `periodo`/`id_empleado`, a period from another branch or nonexistent, or an employee neither from the period's branch nor with a snapshot row (the snapshot rule keeps the detail reachable after a branch change).
- **What it shows**: breadcrumb, period code, Regresar, Anterior/Siguiente, Operativa|Fiscal toggle, employee card (avatar, name, code, puesto, status badge, daily salary of the selected type, period range, payment date), "Percepciones totales" card (fiscal view also shows Gravado/Exento per line + totals). Fiscal only, below: `PayrollDeductionsCard` (ISR line, then IMSS line via `PayrollImssDeduction`) with per-status display rules (`'C'` shows the full breakdown, `'N'` shows the reason, `'X'` says "Recalcula la nómina…" or "No aplica"). Footer: perceptions − deductions = "Neto a pagar". Fiscal, ordinary only, below that: `PayrollCfdiCard` (spec 98) with the stamping status, the Timbrar/Reintentar button or blocked reasons, and the XML/PDF download links once `'T'`.
- **Extraordinary period**: the card shows only the `aguinaldo`/`ptu` line and, in fiscal, `PayrollArticle174IsrCard` replaces `PayrollDeductionsCard`; ordinary lists hidden; a PTU period has no toggle.
- **Perception lines**: built by `buildPerceptionLines(snapshot, fechaIngreso, fechaInicio, commissionTiers, periodKind)` (`lib/payroll/perceptionLines.ts`). Returns `sueldo_base` plus, when `>0`: `comision_consultas`, `comision_tratamientos`, `comision_productos`, `horas_extra_dobles/triples`, `bono_puntualidad` (shown for `'C'`/`'P'`, omitted for `'N'`), `bono_asistencia` (same rule), `bono_extension_jornada`, `prima_vacacional`, `vacaciones_pagadas` — each shown in both payrolls (fiscal also shows taxed/exempt + rule). `sueldo_base` shows the days breakdown (calendar − faltas − retardos) and notes a proportional first period. **New concepts are added here as new lines** — the card iterates the list, no layout change needed.
- **States**: period at status 1 → "Esta nómina aún no se calcula" (no card, no navigation). No snapshot row for the selected type at status 2+ → "Este empleado no está en la nómina {tipo} de este periodo" (toggle/card stay, salary "—", no navigation).
- **Anterior/Siguiente**: `LAG`/`LEAD` over the same period+type+filters, same `ORDER BY` as Procesar. Disabled `<span>` at either end; hidden if the employee isn't in the filtered set.
- **Components** (`procesar/[id_empleado]/componentes/`, all Server Components): `PayrollEmployeeProfileCard`, `PayrollPerceptionsCard`, `PayrollDeductionsCard` (fiscal only) with `PayrollImssDeduction`, `PayrollArticle174IsrCard` (fiscal, extraordinary only) with shared `PayrollCalculationRow`, `PayrollPaidTreatmentsList`, `PayrollSoldProductsList`, `PayrollOvertimeDaysList`, `PayrollAbsencesList`, `PayrollLatenessList` (all hidden when empty), `PayrollTypeToggle`, `PayrollEmployeeNavigation`, `PayrollDetailNotice`. `EmployeeAvatar` lives in `empleados/componentes/`, shared with the employee record header.
